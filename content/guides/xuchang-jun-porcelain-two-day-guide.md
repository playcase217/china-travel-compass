Xuchang makes a useful two-day base for travelers interested in both Three Kingdoms memory and the living ceramic culture of nearby Yuzhou. The strongest plan separates those stories: spend one day reading the city, then give a full day to Jun porcelain and Shenhou Ancient Town rather than squeezing a distant craft stop between urban sights.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** ceramics, craft process, museums, and interpreted historical memory.
- **Time needed:** two days, with one dedicated Yuzhou or Shenhou day.
- **Simple plan:** Xuchang museum and historic core; Shenhou old town and kiln culture.
- **Main planning issue:** Xuchang, Yuzhou, and Shenhou are separate transport stages.
- **Editorial view:** study fragments, kilns, and process before shopping for a finished piece.

## Day one: Xuchang's historical layers

Start with Xuchang Museum or another official city museum after confirming its opening day and passport procedure. Use the collections to separate archaeology and documented local history from later popular narratives about the Three Kingdoms.

Walk one compact central route afterward. Memorial architecture and themed commercial districts can be enjoyable, but their value lies in showing commemoration and modern storytelling. Do not assume every structure is original to the period it evokes.

## Day two: Shenhou and Jun porcelain

Travel early to Shenhou (神垕) in Yuzhou. The town's ceramic identity is not just decorative branding: Xuchang's current Jun Porcelain protection regulation defines the material and firing tradition and calls for protection and interpretation of kiln sites, Shenhou, and related cultural resources.

Prioritize an official museum or kiln-site display before a shop. Look for explanations of clay preparation, forming, glazing, kiln atmosphere, firing, and the variability of glaze effects. A workshop demonstration or hands-on session may require advance booking and may not run every day.

Buy with restraint. Ask whether an object is studio work, a reproduction, or a mass-produced souvenir; request a receipt and written material description for an expensive piece. Never remove ceramic fragments from an archaeological area.

## Transport and practical planning

- Confirm your long-distance train on the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Xuchang's government has published direct-bus information for Shenhou, but timetables and fares are changeable; recheck the current notice or ask the bus station on the day.
- Save 神垕古镇, 禹州, and your return point in Chinese.
- Leave a generous return buffer and have a taxi or ride-hailing fallback that your hotel has helped verify.
