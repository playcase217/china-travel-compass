Karamay and Urho reveal a side of northern Xinjiang shaped by wind, sediment, oil history, and enormous distances. The best-known stop is World Ghost City, a managed scenic area of yardang landforms in Urho District—not an abandoned city and not a place for unsupervised off-road wandering.

Use the first day to understand Karamay and prepare transport. Devote the second to Urho with a confirmed return. The landscape is exposed, and weather and distance should control the schedule.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** geology, desert photography, road-trip planning, and travelers crossing northern Xinjiang.
- **Time needed:** two days, or longer when connecting to another distant destination.
- **Simple plan:** Karamay orientation; a full Urho and World Ghost City excursion.
- **Main planning issue:** long transfers, strong sun and wind, and limited flexibility after late afternoon.
- **Editorial view:** one well-planned landform visit is better than collecting widely separated photo stops.

## Day one: Karamay and logistics

Start with a confirmed-open city museum, exhibition, or public landscape that explains Karamay's petroleum-era development and regional geology. Check identity-document procedures, reservations, and opening days before going. Displays may have limited English, so save the names 克拉玛依 for Karamay and 乌尔禾 for Urho.

Arrange the next day's transport before evening. Confirm whether you are staying in Karamay city or Urho, the exact World Ghost City entrance, waiting arrangements, and the return destination. A map pin without a Chinese name is not enough.

Carry water, sun protection, a windproof layer, closed shoes, and offline destination details. Mobile coverage can vary during long road transfers, and temperatures can shift sharply between sun and evening.

## Day two: World Ghost City and Urho

The Karamay government describes World Ghost City as the core of Urho's yardang tourism landscape. Follow the managed visitor route and use only currently authorized stops and transport. Do not leave the public area to climb formations or walk into the surrounding desert.

Wind and erosion are the subject here. Look at the repeated ridges, grooves, isolated forms, and the scale of the open basin rather than searching only for formations named after familiar objects. Respect barriers: the same soft materials that make the landscape dramatic also make it vulnerable.

Before adding another Urho attraction, calculate the real driving and visiting time. The district promotes multiple sites, but that does not make them adjacent. Drop the extra stop if it threatens your confirmed return or pushes outdoor time into unsafe conditions.

## Safety and responsible access

Never improvise a desert hike, drone flight, or off-road route. Follow local rules, aviation restrictions, fire controls, and staff directions. Tell your lodging or driver the intended route and keep enough water for delays.

In wind, protect eyes and cameras. In extreme heat or cold, shorten the visit. For official forecasts and warnings, check the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) and local notices.

## Transport planning

- Search trains on the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/) and verify the station printed on the ticket.
- Confirm current road conditions and travel times locally; northern Xinjiang distances are easy to underestimate.
- Agree on car price, waiting time, tolls, entrance, and return point before departure.
- Keep the following day flexible if continuing toward another remote part of Xinjiang.
