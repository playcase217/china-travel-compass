Huanglong is a protected mountain valley of travertine pools, waterfalls, forest, and limestone formations—not a quick add-on that should be forced between transport connections. UNESCO places the property in the Minshan Mountains of northwestern Sichuan, where elevation, weather, and conservation rules shape every visit.

Allow two nights around a confirmed gateway. Use the first day to arrive and assess how you feel; give the main valley a flexible second day.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** geology, forests, mountain scenery, and conservation-minded walking.
- **Time needed:** one arrival and acclimatization day, then one full scenic-area day.
- **Main planning issue:** elevation, seasonal operations, weather, road transfers, and entry arrangements can change.
- **Editorial view:** a shorter safe walk is better than completing a checklist while unwell.

## Day one: arrive without rushing

Confirm the Chinese name and exact address of your lodging, the entrance you will use, and the final road transfer. Huanglong, Songpan, and Jiuzhaigou are distinct places; a route that looks compact on a regional map can still require substantial mountain travel.

Keep the arrival day light. Hydrate normally, avoid heavy exertion and alcohol, eat, rest, and notice symptoms rather than dismissing them. Anyone with heart, lung, blood, pregnancy, or previous altitude concerns should seek personalized medical advice before the trip. Headache, nausea, dizziness, unusual fatigue, or breathlessness can be warning signs; worsening symptoms require stopping, alerting a companion or staff member, and seeking medical help or descending as directed. Supplemental oxygen is not a license to continue upward.

## Day two: follow one confirmed route

Check the official forecast, opening notice, transport arrangement, and last-entry time that morning. Cableways, internal transport, boardwalk sections, and seasonal facilities may not all operate. Take warm and waterproof layers even when the gateway is mild.

Use only open boardwalks and signed paths. The colored pools are active and fragile carbonate formations. Never step off route, touch the water, throw coins, collect deposits, use soap, or fly a drone without explicit authorization. UNESCO also records important forest habitat and threatened wildlife; never feed, call, crowd, or pursue an animal.

Move slowly and let symptoms, not a target viewpoint, decide the turnaround point. Build enough time to descend before closing and before mountain-road visibility deteriorates.

## Transport and onward planning

- Save Huanglong as 黄龙 and Songpan as 松潘.
- Check trains only on the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Confirm airport operations directly with the airline and airport; mountain weather can disrupt plans.
- Treat [Jiuzhaigou](/guides/jiuzhaigou-first-time-guide/) as a separate stay, not a guaranteed same-day combination.

## Official sources and planning references

- [UNESCO World Heritage Centre: Huanglong Scenic and Historic Interest Area](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/638)
- [China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/)

Tickets, identity checks, cableways, roads, trails, seasonal opening, and transport can change. Verify current details with the official scenic-area channel, your accommodation, transport operators, and 12306 before traveling.
