Lingling, the historic core of Yongzhou, rewards travelers who like literature to stay attached to real streets and landscapes. The strongest two-day plan centers on Liuzi Temple and Liuzi Street, then widens to East Hill, the riverfront, and an authorized stone-inscription or courtyard site.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** Chinese literature, old streets, temples, inscriptions, and slow urban walking.
- **Time needed:** two days based in Lingling rather than commuting from another district.
- **Simple plan:** Liu Zongyuan context and old city; East Hill and one additional heritage site.
- **Main planning issue:** “Yongzhou” covers separate urban districts and a large prefecture.
- **Editorial view:** read Liu Zongyuan's writing with the landscape, without treating every later memorial as Tang-period fabric.

## Day one: Liuzi Street and the river city

Start at Liuzi Temple after checking the current opening day and passport procedure. Use official interpretation to understand Liu Zongyuan's years in Yongzhou, his writing, and the long history of later commemoration. Then walk Liuzi Street toward the old-city and river areas at a patient pace.

Read plaques carefully: a historic street can include protected structures, restorations, new businesses, and performance spaces. Evening shows, bonfires, and costume activities mentioned in older promotions are programming, not guaranteed daily features.

## Day two: East Hill and one deeper stop

Visit the officially open parts of East Hill, where religious buildings and city views add another layer to Lingling's history. Temples remain cultural or devotional spaces; keep voices low, ask before photographing interiors or people, and obey closures.

For the afternoon, choose one verified excursion. A protected courtyard, a museum, or an authorized inscription site is more realistic than racing across the wider prefecture. Stone carvings must not be touched, rubbed, chalked, or photographed with equipment that obstructs the site. Riverbanks and rock paths can become slippery after rain.

## Practical planning

- Search 零陵 for Lingling and 柳子庙 for Liuzi Temple.
- Check your exact station on the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/); Yongzhou's districts are not interchangeable.
- Confirm that accommodation can register foreign guests and note its Chinese address.
- Carry water, rain protection, your passport, and a payment backup.

This guide can follow [Changsha](/guides/changsha-two-day-itinerary/), but only after checking the actual train and urban transfer times.
