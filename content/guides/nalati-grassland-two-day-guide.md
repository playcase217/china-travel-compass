Nalati deserves two days because its meadow, river-valley, forest, and mountain views are spread across managed routes. Choose one route each day, confirm the current transport system, and leave room for Tianshan weather rather than buying every optional activity.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** subalpine meadow, forest, broad landscapes, and a slower Ili Valley journey.
- **Time needed:** two full days after reaching Nalati town.
- **Simple plan:** one principal scenic route; a second route or low-key local day.
- **Main planning issue:** route names, internal vehicles, and self-drive access can change.
- **Editorial view:** scenery is the main event; optional rides and flights should not crowd out it.

## Day one: learn the managed route system

Start at the official visitor center and choose a single operating route based on weather and walking ability. Government visitor information has described separate grassland and valley routes, but do not use an old list as a live timetable. Confirm the day's stops, last return, transfers, and whether any segment requires a separate booking.

Stay on signed paths and roads. Grassland is not empty recreational space: it includes habitat, grazing land, water sources, and community livelihoods. Do not chase livestock, enter fenced areas, pick flowers, fly a drone near animals or people, or stage photographs in working spaces without permission.

## Day two: take a contrasting route

Choose a second confirmed-open route with different terrain, or keep the day flexible if cloud, rain, wind, or snow reduces visibility. Carry sun protection and warm layers even in summer; conditions can change quickly with elevation.

Horse riding, rafting, paragliding, helicopters, and similar products are optional commercial activities. Verify the licensed operator, insurance, safety briefing, weather cancellation terms, protective equipment, and total price before paying. Never accept pressure to participate.

## Self-driving and passport checks

Ili Prefecture published a 2026 change moving standard Nalati self-drive reservations online while directing passport holders to a service window for relevant processing. This is precisely the kind of rule that can change again. Read the current official notice and booking terms rather than assuming a Chinese-ID workflow will accept a passport.

Confirm checkpoints, driver-license requirements, vehicle limits, passenger details, and whether your rental agreement permits the route. A reservation for Nalati does not replace any separate legal or rental requirement.

## Getting there

Nalati is in Xinyuan County. Road transfers from other Ili destinations are substantial, so avoid a same-day scenic visit between tight rail or flight connections. Verify current public transport through official local channels and check the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) before each travel day.
