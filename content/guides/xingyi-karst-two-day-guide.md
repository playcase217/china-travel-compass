Xingyi pairs one of southwest China's most legible cone-karst landscapes with a steep river canyon. The scenery is dramatic, but a useful two-day plan depends on rain, trail conditions, scenic shuttles, and realistic transfers rather than a fixed photo checklist.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** geology, landscape photography, walking, and travelers continuing through western Guizhou.
- **Time needed:** two days, with a weather buffer in the wet season.
- **Simple plan:** Wanfenglin and nearby villages; Maling River Canyon on day two.
- **Main planning issue:** rain can change visibility, footing, waterfall flow, and trail access quickly.
- **Editorial view:** slow down enough to distinguish a lived farming landscape from scenic-area viewpoints.

## Day one: Wanfenglin

Start at 万峰林 (Wanfenglin) after checking which entrance, sightseeing vehicle, walking route, and viewpoint are operating. Guizhou's forestry authority explains that the wider Xingyi geopark records a long marine and karst history; the visible peaks are not simply a decorative backdrop but part of that geological story.

Use official paths and respect fields, village homes, and working roads. If cycling or walking beyond the main viewpoints, ask locally about the route and return options first. Drone use, commercial photography, and access to agricultural land may be restricted. Treat community performances and craft demonstrations as hosted cultural encounters, not as permission to photograph anyone without consent.

## Day two: Maling River Canyon

Give 马岭河峡谷 (Maling River Canyon) a separate day. Confirm the open entrance, trail direction, elevator or shuttle status, and closing procedure before setting out. Wet stone, steep stairs, falling water, heat, and thunderstorms can materially change the experience; closed barriers are safety controls, not suggestions.

Choose Wanfeng Lake only as a substitute or extra day. Boat operations and shore access depend on current weather, water management, and operator schedules, so it is not a reliable add-on to a full canyon visit.

## Practical planning

- Save 万峰林, 马岭河峡谷, 兴义南站, and 兴义万峰林机场 in Chinese.
- Verify rail services and the correct station on the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Check the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) forecast and ask the scenic areas about same-day trail status.
- Wear shoes with reliable wet grip and carry water, sun protection, rain protection, and a payment backup.
- Never enter closed canyon sections, river channels, caves, or farmland for a photograph.

Xingyi complements [Guiyang](/guides/guiyang-two-day-itinerary/) or [Anshun and Huangguoshu](/guides/anshun-huangguoshu-two-day-guide/) if current rail or road connections are checked.
