Ma'anshan offers a compact way to read the lower Yangtze through landscape and literature. Caishiji is the essential first stop; a second day can add city context or a deliberate excursion toward Dangtu and Li Bai's later-life heritage without turning the trip into a rushed checklist.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** classical poetry, river scenery, historic towers, calligraphy, and an easy Anhui stop near Nanjing.
- **Time needed:** two days for Caishiji plus one city or county focus.
- **Simple plan:** a full Caishiji day; then museums and waterfront public space, or one prearranged Dangtu excursion.
- **Main planning issue:** Caishiji and Li Bai sites in Dangtu are related but not in one walkable cluster.
- **Editorial view:** read a few translated poems before visiting; the landscape becomes far more meaningful.

## Day one: Caishiji and the Yangtze

Spend most of the day at 长江采石矶文化生态旅游区. The Ministry of Culture and Tourism describes a destination built around Caishiji's river cliffs, wetlands, old town, and cultural sites including Taibai Tower and Sanyuan Cave. Its official site may publish current opening notices, tickets, and temporary restrictions; check these rather than copying an old price or timetable.

Move slowly through the historical sequence. River views, later memorial architecture, inscriptions, and stories about Li Bai overlap, but legend and documented biography are not the same thing. Treat the famous moon-catching tale as part of the site's literary afterlife, not as an uncontested account of the poet's death.

Cliff paths and river edges require care in rain, wind, heat, or flood conditions. Stay behind barriers and do not approach closed stairs or unstable banks for a photograph.

## Day two: choose city context or Dangtu

For a low-logistics day, visit a confirmed-open city museum or cultural venue, then walk a maintained public section of the Yangtze waterfront. Ma'anshan's identity includes modern industry as well as poetry, but factories, rail yards, docks, and operational river infrastructure are not visitor attractions unless an official tour is offered.

Alternatively, arrange a focused visit toward 当涂 (Dangtu) for Li Bai-related heritage. Verify the exact site, opening hours, reservation method, and transport in advance. Do not assume every location promoted on a thematic route is open daily or that ride-hailing will be easy for the return.

## Practical planning

- Check the exact Ma'anshan station and current timetable through [China Railway 12306](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Use the Caishiji scenic area's official notices for current admission, opening, route, and weather information.
- Carry your passport and allow for manual registration at museums or ticket offices.
- Confirm any Dangtu excursion directly and arrange return transport before leaving the city.
- Check the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) and local river or scenic-area notices.
- Treat festivals, evening shows, boats, and temporary exhibitions as optional and changeable.
