Hailar is Hulunbuir's transport and service gateway, while the famous grassland landscapes lie beyond the urban area. Two days allow one contextual city day and one focused outing toward the Morgele River without pretending that an enormous region can be covered quickly.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** first-time grassland visitors, regional history, landscapes, and road-based travel.
- **Time needed:** two days for Hailar plus one nearby grassland direction.
- **Simple plan:** Hailar museum or cultural sites; an authorized Morgele River excursion.
- **Main planning issue:** long distances, seasonal transport, weather, and variable visitor controls.
- **Editorial view:** a short trip should go deeper in one area, not collect distant place names.

## Day one: orient in Hailar

Use 海拉尔 (Hailar) to understand the scale and cultures of Hulunbuir. Choose one current museum or public cultural venue and confirm opening and passport procedures directly; do not fill the day with widely separated monuments. Ask staff or your accommodation which official visitor information channel is active.

Before booking a driver, obtain the next day's route, vehicle details, total price, included stops, waiting time, and return deadline in writing. Avoid unsolicited drivers who promise access across private pasture or protected land.

## Day two: Morgele River direction

Travel toward 呼伦贝尔大草原·莫尔格勒河景区 only after confirming that the scenic area and its transport are operating. Hulunbuir's cultural authority previously announced a direct visitor bus linking the airport, Hailar railway station, coach station, and scenic area, but explicitly said service could change with traffic and seasons. Treat that announcement as route context, not a current timetable.

Use designated roads, boardwalks, shuttles, and viewing areas. Grassland is an ecosystem and a working pastoral landscape: do not drive off-road, cross fences, approach livestock, pick plants, or enter homes without invitation. Ask before photographing residents or ceremonies.

## Practical planning

- Save 海拉尔站, 呼伦贝尔机场, 莫尔格勒河, and 呼伦贝尔大草原 in Chinese.
- Check rail services on the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/) and flights with the operating airline.
- Reconfirm the outbound and return vehicle on the day; keep an offline copy of your hotel's address.
- Carry your passport, payment backup, water, sun protection, windproof layers, and insect repellent in season.
- Check the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) for thunderstorms, strong wind, cold, or fire-risk alerts.
