Tonghua is a compact mountain city in southern Jilin with a more serious story than its “wine city” nickname suggests. A balanced first visit pairs the **Yang Jingyu Memorial Cemetery and Northeast Anti-Japanese United Army interpretation** with an urban hill walk and one properly arranged introduction to local wine history.

Keep Ji'an and its UNESCO-listed Koguryo sites for a separate trip. Although Ji'an lies within the wider Tonghua prefecture, forcing it into a city weekend creates long transfers and repeats a destination that deserves its own careful itinerary.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** twentieth-century history, Northeast landscapes, city walks, and industrial or wine heritage.
- **Time needed:** two full days in Tonghua city.
- **Simple plan:** memorial cemetery and city context; Yuhuang Mountain and a confirmed wine-cultural visit.
- **Main planning issue:** memorial security, winter conditions, and factory access can all change.
- **Editorial view:** treat the memorial as a place of remembrance and the winery as an operating workplace, not as interchangeable attractions.

## Day one: memory and city context

Start at the **Yang Jingyu Memorial Cemetery** and its officially open exhibitions. The complex commemorates the commander of the Northeast Anti-Japanese United Army, who died in 1940. Allow time for the historical sequence and for reflection; do not rush through ceremonies, pose casually at burial or memorial spaces, or photograph where staff prohibit it.

Official municipal history records that construction of the cemetery began in 1954 and that it opened in 1957. Look beyond dates to the geography of resistance in severe winter conditions, while remembering that a memorial exhibition has a commemorative purpose. Translation support may be limited, so prepare key names in Chinese and read more than one institutional account.

Later, explore an officially open section of **Yuhuang Mountain Park** or another central public overlook. Paths can be steep, icy, or temporarily closed. Turn back early if lighting, snow, or surface conditions deteriorate.

## Day two: industry, craft, and mountain-city life

Arrange any visit to a wine museum, cellar, or production site in advance through the operator's official channel. Jilin's cultural and tourism authority includes Tonghua wine culture in regional routes, but that does not mean every production building accepts walk-ins. Confirm the legal visitor entrance, tour language, age rules, photography policy, tasting arrangement, and whether the tour is operating on your date.

Treat tastings as optional and modest. Do not drink before driving, hiking, or taking medication that interacts with alcohol. A cultural visit can still be worthwhile without tasting: focus on grape varieties adapted to the region, industrial history, labeling, storage, and how contemporary tourism interprets the city's wine identity.

Use the remaining time for a local market or ordinary neighborhood rather than an unverified remote excursion. Ask before photographing vendors and avoid buying wild plants, animal products, or medicinal claims you cannot evaluate.

## Practical planning

- Check the exact Tonghua station and date on [China Railway 12306](https://www.12306.cn/en/). Service patterns vary seasonally.
- Save 杨靖宇烈士陵园, 玉皇山公园, and your confirmed wine venue in Chinese.
- Winter requires insulated footwear with grip; freeze-thaw conditions can be hazardous even when streets look clear.
- Check the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) for snow, ice, heavy rain, and cold warnings.
- For any factory tour, use only the visitor route and obey hygiene, machinery, smoking, and photography rules.
