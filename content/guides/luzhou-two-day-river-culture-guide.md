Luzhou is a working river city at the meeting of the Yangtze and Tuo rivers, best known nationally for baijiu but more interesting when water, trade, food, and craft are viewed together. Two days allow a compact urban visit without turning the trip into either a factory tour or a rushed excursion across southern Sichuan.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** river-city atmosphere, industrial heritage, museums, Sichuan food, and travelers curious about fermentation.
- **Time needed:** two days in and near central Luzhou.
- **Simple plan:** city museum and waterfront first; one authorized brewing-heritage visit and a green space second.
- **Main planning issue:** tours, tastings, and production access may require reservations and can change for safety or operations.
- **Editorial view:** brewing heritage is worthwhile even for nondrinkers; tasting is optional.

## Day one: understand the river city

Start at **Luzhou Museum** or another official municipal museum. Use the collections to establish the city's position between Sichuan, Chongqing, Yunnan, and Guizhou before walking the central waterfront. Choose an open, well-used riverside section and observe barriers during high water or severe weather.

Continue through central Jiangyang at street level rather than chasing distant attractions. Historic towers, neighborhood food, and views of the river traffic make a coherent afternoon. At night, stay on lit public promenades and do not enter flood-control structures or working docks.

## Day two: learn how a protected craft works

Book an authorized visit to the **Luzhou Laojiao Scenic Area** if brewing history interests you. The important story is the relationship among old cellar spaces, microbial environments, skilled labor, and a living industry. Follow every rule around active production areas, photography, flames, and personal belongings.

Visitors who do not drink can still value the architecture and interpretation. If sampling is offered, never treat it as compulsory, never drive afterward, and check personal health, medication, age, and legal constraints. Families should confirm which sections admit children.

Use the remaining half-day for an officially open park such as Zhangba Longan Forest or another nearby green space, subject to seasonal conditions. Avoid adding remote county attractions unless you have a separate day and confirmed transport.

## Practical planning

- Search 泸州 for Luzhou, 泸州市博物馆 for the city museum, and 泸州老窖景区 for the heritage attraction.
- Ask the attraction directly about passport booking, English interpretation, tour times, child access, and whether production areas are operating.
- Confirm trains on the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/); identify the station before booking accommodation.
- Carry your passport and a payment backup, and use licensed transport after any tasting.
- Check flood, heat, rain, and air-quality notices as well as the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) forecast.

Luzhou combines naturally with [Yibin](/guides/yibin-bamboo-sea-two-day-guide/) or [Chongqing](/guides/chongqing-two-day-itinerary/) when present rail times support the route.
