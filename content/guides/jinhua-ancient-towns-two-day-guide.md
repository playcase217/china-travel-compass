Jinhua is most useful as a base for central Zhejiang, but its heritage places are spread among the urban districts and county-level cities. The common mistake is to treat Youbu, Wuyi, Dongyang, and central Jinhua as adjacent neighborhoods.

For two days, combine the city with one clearly chosen excursion. This produces a calmer trip and leaves room for ordinary street life rather than turning several old towns into a checklist.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** historic streets, regional food, vernacular architecture, and travelers between Hangzhou and southern Zhejiang.
- **Time needed:** two full days.
- **Simple plan:** central Jinhua; either Youbu, Yuyuan, or another confirmed-open heritage town.
- **Main planning issue:** similarly named destinations and transfers across a large prefecture.
- **Editorial view:** one lived-in old town observed patiently is more valuable than three rushed scenic gates.

## Day one: central Jinhua

Begin with a municipal museum or official historic district that is open on your travel date. Use its interpretation to understand why the area was historically known as Wuzhou, then walk a compact sequence of lanes, riverfront space, and local shops.

Do not confuse Jinhua's Wuzhou Ancient Town branding with Wuzhou city in Guangxi. Save the destination in Chinese, keep your hotel address offline, and ask before photographing people, workshops, ceremonies, or private courtyards.

## Day two: choose one town

Youbu Ancient Town in Lanxi is a strong option for travelers interested in a working market-town atmosphere. Yuyuan in Wuyi shifts the emphasis toward a historic village and its architectural ensemble. Official Jinhua tourism pages also list other old settlements, but that is a menu—not a recommendation to combine them all.

Before departure, confirm the correct gate or village entrance, current public access, transport both ways, and whether any building is a residence rather than a visitor attraction. Arrive early enough to walk slowly, buy from local businesses without blocking doorways, and return before transport becomes sparse.

Avoid unsupported stories about a building's age or symbolism unless they appear in reliable on-site interpretation. “Ancient town” is a tourism category, not proof that every visible facade is original.

## Transport and onward planning

- Save Jinhua as 金华, Youbu as 游埠古镇, and Yuyuan as 俞源古村落.
- Check rail journeys on the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Verify whether your train uses Jinhua station or another station in the wider municipality.
- Continue separately to [Hangzhou](/guides/hangzhou-west-lake-one-day-guide/), [Ningbo](/guides/ningbo-two-day-itinerary/), or [Wenzhou](/guides/wenzhou-two-day-itinerary/).
