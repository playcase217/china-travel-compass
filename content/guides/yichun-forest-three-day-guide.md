Yichun is not one compact forest park. It is a widely spread Little Khingan destination whose districts and protected areas require deliberate road planning. In three days, focus on **Wuying's red-pine landscape** and **Tangwanghe's forest-and-granite scenery**, keeping a weather buffer rather than racing toward every attraction on the map.

Summer greenery, autumn color, and winter snow produce very different trips. Never assume a trail, forest road, sightseeing vehicle, or railway connection operates the same way year-round.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** forests, geology, cool summers, autumn color, snow landscapes, and slow nature travel.
- **Time needed:** three days after reaching Yichun; more if arriving overland from Harbin.
- **Simple plan:** orient in the urban service center; Wuying; Tangwanghe.
- **Main planning issue:** long transfers, seasonal operations, wildfire rules, insects, snow, and extreme cold.
- **Editorial view:** choose two protected landscapes and walk them properly. A fast multi-park circuit mostly shows you the inside of a vehicle.

## Day one: arrive and understand the forest city

Use the first day to settle in Yimei or another confirmed transport base, visit an officially open forest-history or natural-history exhibition, and verify the next two days' transfers. Yichun's modern identity is inseparable from forestry and ecological transition; interpretation helps distinguish protected old-growth communities from managed, restored, and urban forest.

Walk only in designated public parks after checking daylight and surface conditions. In winter, short days and severe wind chill make even an urban outing consequential.

## Day two: Wuying and the red-pine ecosystem

Travel to the official **Wuying red-pine forest** visitor area. Provincial sources describe it as a major protected red-pine landscape. Follow boardwalks and staff instructions, keep food secured, and never collect cones, mushrooms, plants, bark, or insects.

Ignore “forest therapy” claims that promise medical outcomes. A forest walk can be restorative, but it does not replace treatment. Visitors with pollen, asthma, mobility, or cardiovascular concerns should plan around their own medical needs and current trail conditions.

## Day three: Tangwanghe's granite and forest

At **Tangwanghe Linhai Qishi**, forest grows among distinctive granite formations. Use the official map and choose a route that fits the day's weather and the last shuttle. Moss, wet leaves, ice, and packed snow can make stone steps unexpectedly slick.

Do not climb formations, leave the trail for photographs, feed squirrels or birds, or approach wildlife. If a trail closes for wind, fire risk, maintenance, or snow, substitute an official indoor or urban stop rather than seeking an unmarked forest entrance.

## Practical planning

- Search Yichun's exact stations and dates on [China Railway 12306](https://www.12306.cn/en/); seasonal services and similarly named cities make assumptions risky.
- Save 伊春, 伊美区, 五营红松林海景区, 丰林县, 汤旺河林海奇石景区, and 汤旺县 in Chinese.
- Prearrange licensed transfers between districts and confirm whether the driver waits; app-car supply can be thin outside town.
- In warm seasons, use tick and mosquito precautions and perform a tick check after walking. In winter, protect exposed skin and carry backup power.
- Check the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) plus current forestry fire, wind, and trail notices.
