Weihai works best as a bay city with a serious maritime-history day, not as a checklist of distant beaches. Make Liugong Island your weather-dependent priority, then use the second day for a flexible urban coast plan.

Stay where you can reach the official tourist wharf without a fragile early connection, and keep a mainland alternative ready if sailings change.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** maritime history, sea views, museums, walking, and a slower Shandong coast stop.
- **Time needed:** two days plus flexibility for marine weather.
- **Simple plan:** Liugong Island; one mainland waterfront and museum day.
- **Main planning issue:** ferry suspension, timed admission, wind, fog, heat, and long distances along the municipal coast.
- **Editorial view:** the island's difficult modern history deserves unhurried museum time rather than serving only as a scenic backdrop.

## Day one: Liugong Island

Check the official scenic-area website and ticket channel before leaving your hotel. Weihai's government identifies the official Liugong Island site and the tourist wharf as the places for current visitor and ticket information. Weather, maintenance, crowd control, or emergency instructions can change sailings.

On the island, prioritize the official museum interpretation of the Sino-Japanese War of 1894–1895 and related naval history, then add a short outdoor route suited to the conditions. Read dates and institutional labels carefully, and avoid turning memorial spaces or military remains into playful photo props.

Follow barriers on cliffs and coastal paths. Do not fly a drone, approach restricted facilities, or assume every historic structure is open.

## Day two: choose one mainland coast

Pick one connected waterfront zone rather than chasing every viral photo point. A city bay walk, an official park, and a local museum or indoor cultural venue make a balanced day. Confirm bathing status before entering the sea; a beach can be visually accessible while swimming is closed or unsafe.

Wind, waves, fog, algae, lightning, and seasonal management affect the coast. Follow flags and staff directions, keep away from slippery breakwaters, and never climb structures for a photograph. In colder months, dress for wind rather than relying on the inland temperature forecast.

## Transport and onward planning

- Save Weihai as 威海, Liugong Island as 刘公岛, and Liugong Island Tourist Wharf as 刘公岛旅游码头.
- Check trains only on the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Verify which Weihai-area station best fits your hotel; similarly named districts and coastal sights can be far apart.
- Continue separately to [Yantai](/guides/yantai-two-day-coastal-itinerary/) or [Qingdao](/guides/qingdao-two-day-itinerary/).
