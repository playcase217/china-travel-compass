Hebi is best used as a base for understanding northern Henan's smaller historic towns rather than as a checklist of scattered attractions. A focused two-day plan gives Xunxian Ancient City and the nearby Grand Canal landscape a full day, then keeps a flexible second day for museums, public heritage spaces, or another verified county stop.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** old streets, canal history, county-town architecture, and travelers continuing through northern Henan.
- **Time needed:** two days.
- **Simple plan:** Xunxian Ancient City and canal context; then a museum-led Hebi day or one confirmed excursion.
- **Main planning issue:** Hebi's districts, rail stations, Xunxian, and Qixian are separate places with road transfers between them.
- **Editorial view:** Xunxian rewards slow observation; do not turn a living county town into a hurried backdrop.

## Day one: Xunxian Ancient City and the canal landscape

Travel to 浚县古城 after confirming the exact drop-off and return. Walk the publicly open streets, city-gate area, and river or canal-side paths without assuming every historic-looking courtyard is a visitor attraction. Xunxian is both a protected historic city and an inhabited commercial center, so keep doorways clear, ask before photographing people, and treat temples and active religious spaces respectfully.

Use the 大运河黎阳仓遗址 (Li Canal and Liyang Granary heritage) interpretation, where currently open, to understand why this place mattered. Official Hebi reporting identifies the granary site and protection-display project as part of the area's Grand Canal story. Archaeological remains may have limited access while conservation or construction is under way; view only from designated public areas.

Seasonal folk events can dramatically change crowds and traffic. Never plan around a festival video without checking the current county notice, reservation or crowd-control measures, and return transport.

## Day two: Hebi context or one carefully chosen extension

Begin with a confirmed-open public museum, cultural center, or city exhibition in Hebi. This provides context for the Qi River region, settlement history, folk traditions, and the modern city's development. Verify the closed day, passport procedure, bag policy, and whether English interpretation is available.

If you prefer a second county excursion, choose one officially open site in Qixian or another nearby area and allow most of the day. Do not combine Xunxian, Qixian, mountain sites, and urban Hebi in a single rushed circuit. County buses and app-based cars may not provide a reliable late return from an outlying entrance.

## Transport and practical planning

Search the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/) for your actual travel date and verify the station printed on the ticket. Save 鹤壁东站, 鹤壁站, 浚县古城, and your exact museum or entrance in Chinese. Ask your accommodation to confirm the road transfer in both directions.

Check the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) for heat, heavy rain, snow, ice, and wind. Old paving can become slippery, while canal edges, construction areas, ruined walls, and unguarded slopes require ordinary caution. Stay outside fenced archaeological and water-control areas.
