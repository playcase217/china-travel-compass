Xilinhot is a practical base for understanding the Xilingol grasslands without reducing the region to a roadside photo stop. A city day supplies museum and religious context; a second day can focus on one legally accessible grassland landscape.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** grassland ecology, Mongolian culture, regional history, and wide landscapes.
- **Time needed:** two days, with a third useful for long road excursions.
- **Simple plan:** museum and Beizi Temple; one confirmed grassland or volcanic-landscape route.
- **Main planning issue:** attractions are spread out and seasonal transport is changeable.
- **Editorial view:** begin with culture and ecology before booking staged entertainment.

## Day one: city context

Start at 锡林郭勒盟博物馆 (Xilingol League Museum), after checking its current notice. The regional cultural authority describes it as a comprehensive museum centered on Mongolian and Yuan-period culture. Exhibitions and hours can change, so do not rely on its older published timetable.

Continue to 贝子庙 (Beizi Temple) if it is officially open. Treat it as a religious place: dress respectfully, keep quiet, and follow photography rules. Use the evening to ask a reputable local operator or your hotel to verify the next day's route, vehicle, access permissions, and return time.

## Day two: choose one grassland direction

Pick one authorized route rather than attempting a vast loop. Possibilities promoted by local authorities include Xilin River landscapes, protected grassland areas, and the Pingdingshan or Baiyinkulun volcanic-grassland direction. Development and conservation boundaries can shift, and some projects may be under construction, so current confirmation is essential.

Remain on public roads and marked visitor areas. Never drive across pasture, enter a fenced enclosure, launch a drone, or photograph residents at close range without permission. If riding is offered, choose an established operator, wear a helmet, and decline when animal welfare or safety looks poor.

## Practical planning

- Save 锡林浩特, 锡林郭勒盟博物馆, 贝子庙, and 平顶山 in Chinese.
- Verify flights or trains with the carrier or the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Agree on route, total price, waiting time, and return point in writing before entering a vehicle.
- Carry your passport, offline maps, sun and wind protection, warm layers, water, and a payment backup.
- Check the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) for wind, thunderstorms, cold snaps, and wildfire-risk conditions.

For an urban introduction to Inner Mongolia, see [Hohhot](/guides/hohhot-two-day-itinerary/); Xilinhot offers a more road-dependent grassland experience.
