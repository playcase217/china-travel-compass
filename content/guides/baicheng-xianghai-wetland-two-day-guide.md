Baicheng is a practical gateway to the wetlands and grasslands of western Jilin, but the landscape should not be approached as a casual roadside safari. Xianghai is an internationally important wetland and a protected habitat. A good two-day trip uses the city as a base, confirms access in advance, and puts wildlife welfare ahead of sightings.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** wetlands, open landscapes, migratory-bird context, and travelers comfortable with advance logistics.
- **Time needed:** two days, with one full day reserved for a confirmed Xianghai visit.
- **Simple plan:** Baicheng city context and trip preparation; then an authorized route at Xianghai.
- **Main planning issue:** Xianghai is in Tongyu County, not central Baicheng, and protected-area access varies by season and conservation needs.
- **Editorial view:** this trip succeeds through patient observation, not through a long species checklist.

## Day one: arrive, orient, and confirm the field day

Use the first day for a confirmed-open city museum, planning venue, or public park, and for final contact with your accommodation, licensed driver, or reserve visitor service. Save the Chinese names 白城市, 通榆县, and 向海国家级自然保护区. A map pin alone is not a safe transport plan.

The city sits within a wider western Jilin landscape of wetlands, grasslands, agriculture, and water-management projects. Official planning documents identify Xianghai and nearby Momoge as core nature reserves, but they are separate destinations. Choose one rather than spending the day racing between them.

Arrange a clear pickup point, return time, vehicle, and fallback plan. Mobile coverage, food options, toilets, shade, and app-based ride availability should not be assumed outside the city.

## Day two: visit Xianghai responsibly

The Convention on Wetlands lists Xianghai as a Wetland of International Importance, and Jilin's official visitor information places it in Xianghai Township, Tongyu County. Before departure, confirm whether ordinary visitors are accepted, which entrance and roads are open, and whether a guide, shuttle, or registration is required.

Stay in designated visitor areas. Never enter a core zone, cross a seasonal closure, play bird calls, approach nests, feed animals, or ask a driver to leave authorized roads. Use binoculars or a long lens from a respectful distance. Drones can disturb wildlife and may be prohibited; do not launch one without explicit authorization.

Bird presence depends on migration, water, wind, temperature, and time of day. Treat cranes and other celebrated species as possibilities, not guaranteed attractions. If conservation staff close an area, accept the closure without searching for an unofficial access point.

## Seasons, transport, and safety

Confirm trains on the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/). Save 白城站 and 白城北站 separately if both appear in your planning tools, and verify which one your service actually uses.

Western Jilin can bring strong wind, dust, heat, thunderstorms, snow, ice, and severe cold. Check the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) and carry water, sun and wind protection, warm layers, and offline destination details appropriate to the season. Do not walk onto frozen water or drive onto unverified tracks.
