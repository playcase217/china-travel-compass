Xichang is a sunny highland city where a lake-and-mountain landscape meets the living cultures of Liangshan. Two days are enough for a focused introduction: devote one day to Qionghai and Lushan, then use the second for Jianchang Ancient City and a carefully chosen museum or cultural venue.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** lakeside walks, birding, regional history, architecture, and learning about Yi culture.
- **Time needed:** two full days; add time rather than squeezing in distant Liangshan destinations.
- **Simple plan:** Qionghai and Lushan; then Jianchang Ancient City and one confirmed-open cultural venue.
- **Main planning issue:** the lake shore, mountain paths, old city, and museums are separate experiences, not one continuous walking route.
- **Editorial view:** treat Xichang as a destination in its own right, not merely a transfer point for Lugu Lake or the wider prefecture.

## Day one: Qionghai and Lushan

Begin at a maintained section of 邛海 (Qionghai) wetland or lakeshore. The lake is part of the nationally recognized Qionghai–Lushan destination, but its visitor areas include multiple gates, wetlands, paths, and transport stops. Choose one section instead of trying to circle the whole shore. Stay on marked routes, keep away from restoration zones, and never disturb birds for photographs.

Continue to 泸山 (Lushan) only if trails and access are officially open. Wildfire prevention, ecological restoration, storms, and maintenance can change what is available. The mountain also contains religious and cultural sites: dress and speak respectfully, do not feed wildlife, and follow photography restrictions. Heat and elevation can make a short-looking climb tiring, so carry water and turn back before weather deteriorates.

## Day two: the old city and cultural context

Walk through 建昌古城 (Jianchang Ancient City) early, when the lanes are calmer. Look for the surviving street pattern and gates, but remember that this is also a commercial and residential district. Night lighting, performances, temporary events, and low-altitude sightseeing products are optional additions whose schedules can change.

Pair the old city with one verified museum or cultural venue. Exhibitions about Liangshan's history, languages, clothing, crafts, and social change deserve time and care. Use the institution's own interpretation, avoid reducing the Yi people to festival imagery, and ask before photographing people, ritual objects, or demonstrations. If your visit coincides with a public festival, check the organizer's current program and crowd controls rather than relying on a previous year's dates.

## Practical planning

- Confirm whether your train uses 西昌西站 and check current services on [China Railway 12306](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Carry the passport used for bookings and allow time for station security and manual registration.
- Check lake-zone entrances, wetland routes, Lushan fire controls, museum opening, and old-city events shortly before visiting.
- Use official taxis, buses, or licensed ride-hailing; save destinations in Chinese.
- Check the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) for heat, rain, wind, and thunderstorms.
- Do not treat the Xichang Satellite Launch Center as a casual city attraction; location, eligibility, identification, and access rules require separate official verification.
