Yunfu offers a quieter western Guangdong trip built around materials and regional history. Begin with the city museum's stone-art and local-history displays, then choose one outlying heritage area rather than trying to connect distant counties in a single day.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** craft, industrial culture, local museums, archaeology, and travelers continuing west from the Pearl River Delta.
- **Time needed:** two days with one planned road excursion.
- **Simple plan:** Yunfu Museum and city context; one Xinxing or other confirmed heritage cluster.
- **Main planning issue:** attractions are dispersed, and workshops, temples, archaeological sites, and scenic areas follow different access rules.
- **Editorial view:** understand stone as geology, skilled work, trade, art, and environmental responsibility—not merely a souvenir.

## Day one: begin with stone and local history

Start at **Yunfu Museum**. The city culture authority describes a stone-art exhibition on the first floor and local-history galleries above it. Confirm the current opening day, exhibition status, reservation channel, and accepted identification.

Look closely at how material, natural pattern, tool marks, design, and polishing affect a finished work. Do not confuse a retail showroom with a museum or an operating factory with a public attraction. Any workshop visit should be arranged through the operator, with explicit safety and photography permission.

Spend the afternoon on an official public route in the city. If visiting a stone market or shop, ask for material identity, dimensions, weight, price, invoice, and shipping terms in writing. Never buy alleged antiquities, fossils, wildlife products, or stone of unclear origin.

## Day two: choose one heritage direction

Yunfu's official tourism planning highlights several distinct themes, including stone culture, Xinxing's Buddhist heritage, the Modao Mountain archaeological site, and the Changgangpo aqueduct. These places are not one compact circuit.

For a first trip, choose a confirmed-open Xinxing cluster or one staffed heritage site. At a working religious site, dress and speak respectfully, follow photography restrictions, and separate historical evidence from promotional origin stories. At an archaeological or engineering site, remain on public paths and do not enter excavations, service areas, or water infrastructure.

Arrange the exact destination and return before leaving Yunfu. A county name in a tourism plan does not guarantee that every highlighted project is completed or open to independent visitors.

## Arrival and onward planning

- Search 云浮, 云浮市博物馆, 新兴县, and the exact chosen site in Chinese.
- Check rail services on the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/) and verify which Yunfu-area station your train uses.
- Check heat, heavy rain, and typhoon information through the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/).
- Continue separately to [Guangzhou](/guides/guangzhou-two-day-itinerary/) or [Zhaoqing](/guides/zhaoqing-dinghushan-two-day-guide/).
