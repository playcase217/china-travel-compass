The Beijing subway is usually the most practical way for international visitors to move around the city. It reaches major districts and attractions, avoids road traffic, and becomes straightforward once you choose a payment method and leave extra time for station security checks.

## At a glance

Use station ticket machines or staffed service centers if you need help, and check current payment options before travel. Bring your passport when required for setup or identity verification. Avoid rush hour when possible, keep your bag easy to screen, and save destination names in both English and Chinese.

## Why use the subway

Beijing is large. Walking alone is not realistic for a multi-day trip, and road traffic can make taxi times unpredictable. The subway gives you a stable backbone for sightseeing days.

Stations and transfers can still be large, so budget time for walking inside the system rather than treating every ride as door-to-door transport.

## Tickets and payment

Options can include single-journey tickets, transit cards, and supported mobile payment methods. The most suitable choice depends on your phone, payment setup, and length of stay.

If a machine is unclear, use the staffed service center. Beijing has expanded foreign-card acceptance and multilingual support, but payment details can change, so verify current instructions after arrival.

## Security checks and rush hour

Expect bag screening before entering the paid area. Keep water bottles and small items easy to show if requested.

Avoid the busiest commuting periods when your schedule is flexible. A less crowded train is worth a small itinerary adjustment, especially if you are carrying bags or traveling with children.

## Make navigation easier

- Save attraction and hotel names in Chinese.
- Check the final train time before a late evening.
- Allow time for long transfers.
- Stand clear of doors and let passengers exit first.
- Use the [airport-to-city guide](/guides/beijing-airport-to-city/) for arrival-day transport.

## Use the subway for sightseeing

The network works well for the [Temple of Heaven](/guides/temple-of-heaven-beijing/), [Summer Palace](/guides/summer-palace-beijing/), and central Beijing areas. For Mutianyu Great Wall, arrange a separate road transport plan.
