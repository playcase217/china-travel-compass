The simplest way to reach central Beijing depends on which airport you use, your arrival time, and your luggage. Airport express trains are usually the best default during operating hours, while official taxis are practical after a long flight or when your hotel is not convenient to the rail network.

## At a glance

Beijing Capital International Airport and Beijing Daxing International Airport are different airports with different express rail connections. Confirm your airport code before departure. Save your hotel's Chinese address, keep a backup payment method, and use official transport signs or service counters after arrival.

## Capital Airport: use the Airport Express

Beijing Capital International Airport is commonly shown as `PEK`. Its Airport Express connects the airport with Sanyuanqiao and Dongzhimen, where you can transfer to the wider subway network.

This works well if you arrive during operating hours, have manageable luggage, and want a predictable trip into the city.

## Daxing Airport: connect through Caoqiao

Beijing Daxing International Airport is commonly shown as `PKX`. Its airport express links the airport with Caoqiao, where onward subway connections are available.

Daxing is farther south, so check the location of your hotel before assuming that the same route advice applies to both airports.

## When a taxi makes sense

Choose an official taxi if you are tired, carrying several bags, arriving late, or staying somewhere inconvenient for rail transfers.

Keep the hotel name and address in Chinese. Do not rely only on an English hotel name or a map pin. Use the official taxi line and ignore unsolicited transport offers inside the terminal.

## Prepare before landing

- Confirm whether your ticket arrives at `PEK` or `PKX`.
- Save the hotel's Chinese name, address, and phone number.
- Keep mobile data ready or save the essentials offline.
- Carry a backup card and some cash.
- Use airport service counters if payment, transport, or communication setup is unclear.
