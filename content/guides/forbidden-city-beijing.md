The Forbidden City is the essential first landmark for understanding Beijing. For international visitors, the best plan is to reserve in advance, arrive early, walk the central axis from south to north, and allow at least three hours without trying to inspect every hall.

## At a glance

Book before your visit: the Palace Museum does not sell same-day tickets at the entrance. Bring the passport used for your reservation. The museum is generally closed on Mondays except on statutory holidays. Start at the Meridian Gate in the south and finish near Jingshan Park in the north for a rewarding view over the palace roofs.

## Why it belongs on your Beijing itinerary

The Forbidden City was the imperial palace of the Ming and Qing dynasties. Its courtyards, ceremonial halls, gates, and rooflines reveal how power and ritual shaped the heart of old Beijing.

Even if you are not a museum specialist, the scale is memorable. The experience is strongest when you focus on the architecture and choose a few side areas rather than rushing through every exhibition room.

## A practical first-time route

Enter through the Meridian Gate and follow the main axis through the great ceremonial halls. Continue north toward the Imperial Garden. If energy and time allow, add one side route for quieter courtyards and a closer look at palace details.

After exiting at the north, walk to nearby Jingshan Park. The short uphill climb gives you one of Beijing's best classic views: the Forbidden City spread out below, with the city extending beyond it.

## When to go

Choose an early entry time if possible. Courtyards feel more spacious, summer temperatures are more comfortable, and you have enough time to explore without watching the clock.

Spring and autumn are especially pleasant. In summer, carry water and expect exposed walking areas. In winter, dress for wind and enjoy clearer views on bright days.

## Booking and entry tips

- Reserve through the official Palace Museum ticketing channels.
- Use your passport details exactly as shown on your travel document.
- Carry the original passport used for the booking.
- Check the official calendar before planning a Monday visit.
- Wear comfortable shoes: the visit involves long walks over stone surfaces.

## Make it a memorable half day

Pair the Forbidden City with [Tiananmen Square](/guides/tiananmen-square-beijing/) before your museum entry, then finish at Jingshan Park. This creates a logical south-to-north route and avoids unnecessary backtracking.
