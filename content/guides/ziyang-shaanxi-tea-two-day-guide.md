Ziyang in southern Shaanxi offers a different image of the province: a steep Han River town connected with tea gardens, local song traditions, and mountain settlements. It rewards travelers who slow down and arrange one verified countryside visit instead of expecting a polished circuit of major sights.

Two days allow a town-and-river orientation plus one tea-focused excursion. Tea harvests, performances, and hands-on activities are seasonal and should never be assumed from festival publicity.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** tea culture, river towns, folk traditions, mountain scenery, and repeat visitors to Shaanxi.
- **Time needed:** two days, with extra time for remote villages.
- **Simple plan:** Ziyang town and cultural context; then one tea area such as Huangu or another officially open route.
- **Main planning issue:** steep streets, winding roads, seasonal programs, rain, and limited English service.
- **Editorial view:** visit tea country to understand land, labor, processing, and song—not to collect unsupported health claims.

## Day one: town, river, and cultural context

Walk a manageable part of Ziyang after confirming safe routes from your accommodation. The relationship among hillside settlement, railway, roads, and the Han River is central to the experience. Use official museums, cultural venues, or current exhibitions to learn about local tea production and Ziyang folk song.

Do not assume a river cruise or evening performance operates daily. Water levels, weather, maintenance, and event calendars can change. View the river only from designated public areas and obey flood-control barriers.

When tasting or buying tea, ask about harvest date, producer, style, storage, and brewing rather than accepting broad medical promises. “Selenium-rich” is part of Ziyang's official tea branding, but tea is not a treatment and individual products should not be represented as curing disease.

## Day two: one tea-town excursion

Choose a single officially promoted area and confirm access through the venue, township, or your accommodation. Huangu has hosted 2026 tea-and-culture programming combining tea gardens, local history, and folk-song activities, but a festival report does not guarantee daily demonstrations.

Arrange transport in both directions before leaving town. At a working garden, remain on invited paths, ask before picking leaves or photographing workers, and do not expect a factory to accept walk-ins. If an experience includes processing or tasting, confirm the operator, fee, duration, and hygiene arrangements directly.

Mountain roads can be affected by heavy rain, fog, landslides, or temporary controls. Follow official warnings and cancel a countryside drive when local authorities advise against travel.

## Practical planning

- Search 紫阳 for Ziyang, 焕古镇 for Huangu, and 紫阳民歌 for Ziyang folk song.
- Confirm the station and current train through the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Choose accommodation that can register foreign guests and explain your next-day route in Chinese.
- Carry your passport, water, rain protection, and a payment backup.

Ziyang can connect with [Hanzhong](/guides/hanzhong-two-day-han-culture-guide/) or a wider [Xi'an](/guides/xian-two-day-city-itinerary/) trip, but verify every current transfer.
