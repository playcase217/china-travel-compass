Qingyuan is Guangdong's largest prefecture-level city by land area, so its attractions do not form one compact sightseeing zone. For a first nature-focused visit, choose **Lianzhou** in the north as your base and build the trip around one karst attraction, nearby streets, and plenty of transport margin.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** caves, river landscapes, smaller-city Guangdong, regional food, and travelers continuing toward Hunan.
- **Time needed:** two days and one night in or near Lianzhou.
- **Simple plan:** settle into Lianzhou and explore locally on day one; visit Lianzhou Underground River on day two.
- **Main planning issue:** “Qingyuan” on an attraction listing may still mean a long road journey from Qingyuan's urban core.
- **Editorial view:** this is a northern Guangdong trip, not an add-on assembled from every attraction in the prefecture.

## Day one: arrive and understand Lianzhou

Travel to **Lianzhou** (连州), check in, and spend the remaining daylight walking a compact part of town. Look for a local museum, historic street, riverfront, or officially open cultural site rather than booking a second distant scenic area. Lianzhou's municipal visitor bureau publishes route suggestions, but individual stops and operating arrangements should be rechecked.

Use dinner to explore northern Guangdong food. Ask what is seasonal and local, and confirm meat, stock, allergens, and spice level rather than relying on translated dish names. Carry the written Chinese address of your hotel: ride-hailing coverage and driver willingness can thin out beyond the center.

## Day two: Lianzhou Underground River

Visit **Lianzhou Underground River** (连州地下河), which official provincial and municipal pages describe as a major cave attraction. Expect a managed scenic route rather than independent caving. Surfaces may be damp, light levels low, and sections may involve steps or a boat component depending on current operations.

Wear shoes with grip and bring a light layer even in warm weather. Follow all photography, boat, helmet, and barrier instructions. Travelers with reduced mobility, vertigo, or sensitivity to enclosed spaces should ask the attraction about the exact current route before buying.

Do not automatically combine the cave with every waterfall, gorge, village, or ethnic-culture attraction promoted under the wider Qingyuan name. Add only a nearby stop that your accommodation or the official attraction confirms fits your return connection.

## Practical planning

- Search 连州 for Lianzhou and 连州地下河 for Lianzhou Underground River.
- Verify the actual boarding point, travel time, and last return for every bus or car leg.
- Confirm rail segments on the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/); road transfers still require separate verification.
- Check rain and severe-weather alerts with the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/).
- Carry your passport and do not assume an online system accepts passport numbers; ask about counter purchase if needed.

If you want a more urban Lingnan trip, choose [Guangzhou](/guides/guangzhou-two-day-itinerary/) or [Foshan](/guides/foshan-lingnan-culture-day-trip/) instead. Lianzhou rewards travelers willing to spend time on the road for a focused landscape visit.
