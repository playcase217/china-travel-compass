Chuzhou makes an easy literary-and-landscape break between larger eastern China cities. Its centerpiece is Langya Mountain, where forest, inscriptions, religious buildings, and the memory of Ouyang Xiu's “Record of the Old Drunkard's Pavilion” belong to one walkable cultural landscape.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** classical literature, low-mountain walking, pavilions, inscriptions, and a quiet overnight stop.
- **Time needed:** two days, with one main mountain day.
- **Simple plan:** arrive and read the text in context; walk one official Langya Mountain circuit.
- **Main planning issue:** separate scenic components may use different tickets, gates, or closing times.
- **Editorial view:** read a good translation before the walk, then notice how later generations built and rebuilt the landscape around the essay.

## Day one: prepare the literary context

Arrive in Chuzhou, save 滁州, 琅琊山, and 醉翁亭 in Chinese, and check the scenic area's current notices. Use the afternoon for a city walk or an official cultural venue rather than squeezing in the mountain near closing time.

Ouyang Xiu wrote the famous pavilion essay while serving in Chuzhou in the Northern Song period. The present visitor landscape is layered: paths, pavilions, inscriptions, memorial spaces, and restored structures come from different periods. Treat the essay as an entry point, not proof that every building visible today survives unchanged from the eleventh century.

## Day two: walk one official circuit

The official scenic-area site groups Zuiweng Pavilion, Tongle Garden, South Gate viewpoints, Shenxiu Lake, Langya Temple, and Rang Spring within the main visitor landscape. Select a circuit that fits current openings and your mobility. Wet stone, summer heat, autumn crowds, and winter ice can make a modest hill more demanding than its elevation suggests.

At Zuiweng Pavilion and the inscription areas, do not touch or make rubbings from stone, lean on railings, or crowd narrow passages for photographs. At Langya Temple, keep voices low, follow photography signs, and do not interrupt worship.

Stay on maintained paths. Forest fire rules, storms, maintenance, and conservation work can close sections. A locked path is not an invitation to improvise through woodland.

## Getting there and combining the trip

Chuzhou is connected with the Nanjing and wider Yangtze River Delta transport network, but station names and services differ. Verify the exact station and train on the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/). Do not assume a fast train station is beside the mountain gate.

Check the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) and the official scenic notice on the morning of the walk. Carry water, rain protection, and shoes with grip.
