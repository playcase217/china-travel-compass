Chaozhou is a compact but deep stop for travelers interested in Teochew food, ritual architecture, opera, carving, and a lived-in old city. Its headline sight is Guangji Bridge, yet the better trip connects the bridge with the lanes, temples, workshops, and meals around it.

Stay two nights near—but not necessarily inside—the busiest old-city streets. Spend day one on the historic core and day two on museums, craft context, and a quieter neighborhood.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** regional food, historic streets, temples, crafts, and slow walking.
- **Time needed:** two days.
- **Simple plan:** old city and Guangji Bridge first; culture and craft depth second.
- **Main planning issue:** pedestrian crowds and bridge access vary by day and event.
- **Editorial view:** Chaozhou works best as a cultural city, not a checklist of snack shops.

## Day one: old city to the Han River

Begin early in the old city, using Paifang Street as an orientation spine rather than the whole experience. Turn into side lanes, remain considerate around homes and active places of worship, and look for details in stone, wood, rooflines, and shopfronts.

Continue to Guangji Bridge after checking that day's admission and operating notice. The historic bridge incorporates multiple structural forms and its visitor arrangements can be affected by maintenance, water conditions, holidays, or special events. Do not promise yourself a particular opening or evening display without official confirmation.

Crossing or viewing the Han River makes a natural endpoint. In hot or wet weather, schedule a long midday rest and return after conditions improve.

## Day two: temples, museums, and food with context

Choose one or two cultural anchors rather than collecting every landmark. Kaiyuan Temple is an active religious site: dress respectfully, keep voices low, and follow photography restrictions. An official museum or heritage display can provide context for Chaozhou woodcarving, ceramics, embroidery, music, and opera.

Treat food as part of the itinerary. Try a deliberate mix of a sit-down meal, tea, and small snacks instead of grazing continuously. Ask prices before ordering seafood or ingredients sold by weight, and mention allergies in writing. “Teochew cuisine” is broad; one meal cannot represent it all.

If considering an outlying village or workshop, arrange it only after confirming opening, permission, and transport. A working studio is not automatically a walk-in attraction.

## Arrival and practical planning

Chaoshan Railway Station serves the wider region and is not in Chaozhou's old city. Chaozhou and neighboring Shantou also share the regional airport. Check the exact terminal or station and transfer on the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/) and official airport channels.

- Save Chaozhou as 潮州 and Guangji Bridge as 广济桥.
- Carry the passport used for bookings.
- Check bridge, temple, and museum notices on the day.
- Allow a heat or rain break at midday.
- Ask before photographing worship or craft work.
- Confirm prices and units before ordering.
