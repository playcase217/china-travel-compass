Wanning is not one continuous resort beach. Riyue Bay, Shimei Bay, and the Shenzhou Peninsula area have different access, accommodation, and water conditions, and conditions change by the hour. For a two-day trip, stay in one bay area and visit at most one other.

If you are new to surfing, book a qualified instructor and judge the lesson by safety practice—not promises that a beach is always “beginner friendly.”

## At a glance

- **Best for:** coast time, surfing, cafés, tropical scenery, and a slower Hainan stop.
- **Time needed:** two days plus a weather buffer in storm season.
- **Simple plan:** orientation and supervised lesson; one second bay or a dry-land coastal day.
- **Main planning issue:** marine conditions, heat, typhoons, rip currents, lesson quality, and road transfers.
- **Editorial view:** the right session for today's sea is better than chasing a famous break beyond your ability.

## Choose a responsible base

Select lodging near the bay where you expect to spend most of your time. Confirm the exact Chinese address, legal guest registration, walking access to food, and transport after dark. Do not assume two places labeled “Wanning” are close together.

Before paying for a surf lesson, ask about instructor qualifications and student ratio; swimming requirements; board, leash, and rash protection; weather cancellation; insurance; emergency communication; and how the instructor assesses currents and hazards. A responsible school should be willing to cancel or relocate a lesson.

## Day one: learn the bay

Walk the public beach in daylight and identify lifeguarded or managed zones, warning flags, rocks, channels, boat routes, and exit points. Take a lesson only where the instructor judges conditions suitable. Tell them honestly about swimming ability, injuries, and medication.

Never surf alone as a beginner, enter during a closure, attach a board leash around your hand, or rely on an inflatable toy as safety equipment. Protect yourself from sun and dehydration even on cloudy days.

## Day two: adapt to the forecast

If conditions remain suitable, take a second supervised session or practice only within the instructor's limits. Otherwise choose a coastal walk, café, village meal, or another authorized dry-land activity. A cancelled water day is a sound outcome when lightning, strong wind, poor water quality, large surf, or a tropical system is present.

Leave shells, coral, animals, and fishing equipment where they are. Use reef-conscious habits, refill water, secure litter, and ask before photographing residents or working fishers.

## Transport and onward planning

- Save Wanning as 万宁, Riyue Bay as 日月湾, Shimei Bay as 石梅湾, and Shenzhou Peninsula as 神州半岛.
- Check trains only on the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
