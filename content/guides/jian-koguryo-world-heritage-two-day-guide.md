Ji'an in southern Jilin is one of China's most important archaeological destinations. UNESCO's serial property links Guonei City, Wandu Mountain City, royal and noble tombs, and the Gwanggaeto Stele to the capitals and funerary culture of the ancient Koguryo kingdom. Two days let you see the main Ji'an components without turning a protected landscape into a checklist.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** archaeology, ancient urbanism, inscriptions, tomb architecture, and Northeast Asian history.
- **Time needed:** two full days after reaching Ji'an.
- **Simple plan:** museum and plain-city remains; then the mountain city and selected tomb or stele sites.
- **Main planning issue:** the World Heritage property is serial and dispersed, while open components and visitor circulation can change for conservation.
- **Editorial view:** begin with interpretation, then visit fewer monuments with better historical context.

## Day one: museum, Guonei City, and the river valley

Start at a confirmed-open museum or archaeological-site visitor center. The terminology varies across English-language sources—Koguryo, Goguryeo, and Gaogouli all occur—so use the museum to establish a timeline before walking the remains.

Continue to 国内城 (Guonei City), a plain-city site now embedded in modern Ji'an. UNESCO identifies Guonei and Wandu as a paired capital system, one on the plain and one in the mountains. Surviving walls and archaeological zones are protected evidence, not scenery to climb. Follow the marked route and do not remove stones, make rubbings, or step into active research areas.

Finish with an official public viewpoint over the Yalu River valley if open. Ji'an is a border city: photograph only where permitted, obey signs and staff, keep away from patrol, port, railway, and military infrastructure, and do not use a drone.

## Day two: Wandu Mountain City and royal monuments

Devote the morning to 丸都山城 (Wandu Mountain City) if the official route is open. Its landscape setting is central to understanding the capital, but steep or exposed sections may close because of rain, snow, ice, fire risk, excavation, or restoration. Wear footwear suited to uneven ground and turn back when barriers are in place.

In the afternoon, select officially open monuments such as the General's Tomb and the Gwanggaeto Stele rather than trying to reach every mapped tomb. Many tombs are conservation sites with no public interior. Wall paintings are especially vulnerable; never assume that a photograph seen online represents present visitor access.

## Practical planning

- Search for the precise Ji'an station and current services on [China Railway 12306](https://www.12306.cn/en/); do not confuse Ji'an in Jilin with Ji'an in Jiangxi.
- Verify which heritage components share a ticket, require reservations, or use scenic transport.
- Carry your passport and allow for security and manual registration.
- Use licensed local transport and agree on the return journey before leaving town.
- Check the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) for rain, snow, ice, heat, and mountain fire conditions.
- Follow all border, photography, drone, archaeological, and conservation restrictions.
