Taizhou in Jiangsu is a compact alternative to the province's busier headline cities. Its center combines an old moat, gardens, opera heritage, historic institutions, and a strong breakfast culture; nearby Qintong adds a small water town and wetland landscape without pretending to be a second city center.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** waterside walking, gardens, opera history, breakfast culture, and low-key old towns.
- **Time needed:** two days; spend one centrally and one around Qintong or another single excursion.
- **Simple plan:** Fengcheng River and central heritage; then Qintong Ancient Town with an optional wetland visit.
- **Main planning issue:** Taizhou, Taizhou in Zhejiang, and nearby county-level destinations are easy to confuse.
- **Editorial view:** use the full label “Taizhou, Jiangsu” in every booking and map search.

## Day one: the old moat and cultural city

Begin around the 凤城河 (Fengcheng River). The municipal government describes it as a substantially preserved ancient city moat whose banks gather sites related to local history, opera, folklore, and commerce. Choose a walk that connects Wanghailou, the old street, and one garden or museum rather than trying to enter every attraction.

Opera enthusiasts should check the current program and opening status of the Mei Lanfang Memorial Museum. Even without a performance, the collection can introduce the life and stage world of the Taizhou-born Peking opera artist. Follow venue rules for photography of costumes, documents, and temporary exhibitions.

Start early enough to try Taizhou breakfast at a reputable restaurant. Ask about fillings and allergens; crab roe products, shrimp, pork, gluten, sesame, and hot broth are common considerations. Posted promotions and scenic-area discounts are temporary, so never build the trip around an old offer.

## Day two: Qintong, with limits

Travel to 溱潼古镇 (Qintong Ancient Town), which the city describes as a historic settlement surrounded by water, with stone-paved lanes, traditional buildings, old trees, and small interpretation venues. Keep your route compact and remember that homes and community spaces are not photo sets.

If time, weather, and confirmed transport permit, pair the town with the nearby Qinhu wetland attraction. Check whether admission, boats, internal transport, or conservation areas are operating that day. Otherwise, spend longer in the town and return to central Taizhou without rushing.

Do not add Xinghua's water forest or seasonal rapeseed fields to the same day: they are separate journeys whose appeal and access depend heavily on season.

## Practical planning

- Confirm the province and exact station name before buying rail tickets on [China Railway 12306](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Ask your hotel to write “江苏泰州” and the destination in Chinese for drivers.
- Verify museum closure days, river cruise schedules, last entry, and wetland boat operations.
- In high heat or rain, replace the midday moat walk with a museum or garden rest.
- Stay behind barriers along waterways and supervise children closely near steps and docks.
- Reserve the return from Qintong before relying on a ride-hailing pickup.
