Quzhou offers a quieter Zhejiang city break built around the southern branch of Confucius's descendants, a compact historic core, and access to very different outer-area landscapes. For two days, keep the first day in the old city and choose only one excursion on the second.

This is not “Qufu in the south.” Learn why the lineage moved and how local history developed on its own terms.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** Confucian history, old-city walks, regional museums, and travelers crossing western Zhejiang.
- **Time needed:** two days.
- **Simple plan:** Southern Confucian heritage and old city; one museum or outer-area excursion.
- **Main planning issue:** venue reservations, closed days, restoration work, and distances to Longyou or Jiangshan.
- **Editorial view:** Quzhou is strongest as a city of living regional culture, not a hurried gateway to scattered attractions.

## Day one: understand the southern lineage

Begin at the Kong Family Southern Ancestral Temple or its current official visitor route, then use a nearby museum or interpretation center to add historical context. Official cultural sources connect the site with the descendants who moved south during the Song period and with Quzhou's continuing Southern Confucian identity.

Treat the complex as a heritage and ritual place. Dress and speak respectfully, do not interrupt ceremonies, and ask before photographing worship or educational activities. Verify whether the temple, individual halls, and nearby institutions use separate admission or reservation systems.

Continue through the old city toward a confirmed-open historic street or wall area. Look for everyday urban life as well as restored façades, and avoid entering residential courtyards without permission.

## Day two: city depth or one excursion

If you prefer a slow trip, spend the second morning at Quzhou Museum and continue to another old-city cultural venue. Otherwise choose one outer-area destination—such as Longyou's grotto visitor site or Jiangshan's Jianglang Mountain—only after checking official access, weather, and realistic transfers.

Do not combine both in one day. They lie in different county-level areas, and a map of “Quzhou attractions” can disguise substantial travel. At caves or mountains, follow the designated visitor route and accept closures for conservation or weather.

## Transport and onward planning

- Save Quzhou as 衢州 and Kong Family Southern Ancestral Temple as 孔氏南宗家庙.
- Check trains only on the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Confirm the exact station and attraction in Chinese before using a taxi or licensed car.
- Continue separately to [Hangzhou](/guides/hangzhou-west-lake-one-day-guide/) or [Wuyuan](/guides/wuyuan-villages-two-day-guide/).
