If your passport is lost or stolen in China, first get to a safe place, report the loss to local police, contact your own embassy or consulate, and follow the National Immigration Administration's instructions for any Chinese exit-entry document you need. Do not book a replacement journey until the issuing authorities confirm which documents will be valid.

## At a glance

- **Immediate danger or theft in progress:** call police at 110.
- **Report the loss:** ask the local public security authority how to obtain written confirmation of the report.
- **Contact your embassy or consulate:** only your country can explain its emergency-passport process.
- **Contact Chinese immigration:** NIA's 12367 service or the local exit-entry administration can explain the Chinese-side procedure.
- **Pause nonessential travel:** hotels, trains, flights, and attractions may require the original document or an officially accepted replacement.

## Step one: protect yourself and your accounts

If a bag was stolen, move somewhere safe and call 110. Freeze affected bank cards and payment accounts through official channels. Do not chase a thief, pay an unofficial “recovery” service, or share verification codes.

Write down where and when you last saw the passport, the document number if known, and what else is missing. Digital copies do not replace a passport, but a secure copy can help authorities identify the document.

## Step two: make a police report

Report the loss or theft to the police authority responsible for the location. Ask what written confirmation will be issued and check names, document number, date, and place for errors before leaving. A hotel or host may help communicate, but use an official police channel.

The exact office and paperwork can vary. The National Immigration Administration's guidance refers to confirmation of a passport-loss report in later exit-entry procedures, so keep every original and scan it securely.

## Step three: contact your embassy or consulate

Use your foreign ministry's official website or China's Ministry of Foreign Affairs directory to find the correct mission. Ask what is required for an emergency passport or other travel document, whether an appointment is needed, how long the process may take, and where the document can be used.

Requirements differ by nationality. You may need identity evidence, photographs, the police report, proof of citizenship, travel plans, a fee, or a contact who can confirm identity. Never rely on another country's checklist.

## Step four: complete the Chinese exit-entry process

A replacement passport from your embassy may not by itself recreate a lost Chinese visa, stay permit, or residence permit. Contact the local exit-entry administration and explain the full situation.

NIA publishes procedures for reissuing permits and, in defined circumstances, issuing a foreigner's exit-entry permit when a person cannot obtain a replacement international travel document. These are case-specific government processes—not interchangeable shortcuts. Bring the police confirmation, embassy documents, new travel document if issued, photographs, and any other material requested.

## Step five: rebuild the rest of the trip

Tell the accommodation provider because its registration record may need updating. Contact airlines, railway operators, and ticket sellers before changing bookings. China Railway's official visitor guidance identifies certain temporary documents that may be accepted, but acceptance depends on the exact document and transaction.

Do not assume a passport photocopy, embassy receipt, police report, or old booking will pass an airport or railway identity check. Obtain confirmation from the responsible operator and authority, then allow extra time.

## Prepare before a problem

- Store an encrypted passport copy separately from the original.
- Save your embassy's official emergency contact details.
- Keep your visa or entry record, accommodation address, insurance policy, and ticket references accessible.
- Share a basic itinerary with a trusted person.
- Carry the passport securely and avoid handing it to anyone without a legitimate reason.
