Three days in Beijing is enough for a strong first introduction if you group sights geographically and avoid overloading each day. Prioritize one major experience each morning, leave room for transport and security checks, and treat the Great Wall as a dedicated day trip.

## At a glance

Use day one for Tiananmen Square, the Forbidden City, and a hutong walk. Give day two to Mutianyu Great Wall. Use day three for the Temple of Heaven and Summer Palace, adjusting the pace if you prefer a longer neighborhood or food experience.

## Day 1: Beijing's historic center

Start with [Tiananmen Square](/guides/tiananmen-square-beijing/) and continue north into the [Forbidden City](/guides/forbidden-city-beijing/). These require separate reservations, so arrange them before arrival and leave extra time for identity and security checks.

After the palace, climb Jingshan Park if you still have energy. Later, shift to a slower [Shichahai hutong walk](/guides/beijing-hutong-walk/) and dinner.

## Day 2: Mutianyu Great Wall

Keep the second day focused on the [Mutianyu Great Wall](/guides/mutianyu-great-wall/). Travel time, mountain walking, and changing weather make it a poor day to combine with another major ticketed landmark.

Leave early, wear shoes with grip, and choose a cable car option if it lets you spend more of your energy on the wall itself.

## Day 3: Temple of Heaven and Summer Palace

Visit the [Temple of Heaven](/guides/temple-of-heaven-beijing/) in the morning for architecture and local park life. After lunch, continue to the [Summer Palace](/guides/summer-palace-beijing/) for a slower lakeside finish.

If that feels too ambitious, choose only one and spend the rest of the day on food, neighborhoods, or a relaxed departure schedule.

## Where to stay

For a short first trip, prioritize subway access over a highly specific neighborhood recommendation. Central areas with convenient transfers reduce friction when your itinerary includes both historic sights and a Great Wall pickup point.

## Before you arrive

- Reserve the Forbidden City and Tiananmen Square separately.
- Confirm the Great Wall transport plan.
- Prepare mobile data and payment backups.
- Save hotel and attraction names in Chinese.
- Check opening days before locking the itinerary.

## Make the trip your own

Three days is a framework, not a race. Drop one attraction if you want longer meals, photography, or neighborhood time. Read the [first trip to China guide](/guides/first-trip-to-china/) for the setup that makes independent travel easier.
