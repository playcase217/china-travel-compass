Fuxin tells an unusually broad story: prehistoric Chahai, agate craft, Mongolian cultural landscapes, and a coal city confronting the legacy of a vast open-pit mine. Two days work best when the urban industrial story comes first and the second day is reserved for one verified excursion.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** industrial heritage, archaeology, urban transformation, craft traditions, and travelers exploring inland Liaoning.
- **Time needed:** two days for the city plus one outlying destination.
- **Simple plan:** museum and Haizhou mining-heritage context; then Chahai or Haitang Mountain.
- **Main planning issue:** the mine, Chahai, Haitang Mountain, agate markets, and other sites do not form one compact circuit.
- **Editorial view:** choose archaeology or mountain culture on day two; doing both usually sacrifices depth and safe transport margins.

## Day one: coal, city, and transformation

Begin at a confirmed-open city museum or industrial-heritage exhibition, then visit an officially accessible viewing or interpretation area for 海州露天矿 (Haizhou open-pit mine). Municipal sources identify the site as an important industrial-heritage destination, but a heritage label does not make the entire mine safe or public.

Obey every fence and warning. Mine edges, spoil heaps, rail lines, machinery, subsidence areas, and remediation works can be dangerous. Never enter through an informal path or ask a driver to stop inside a restricted zone. Use only maintained public viewpoints and current official visitor routes.

If time remains, explore Fuxin's agate tradition through a reputable market, museum, or workshop that welcomes visitors. Ask about material origin and treatment, compare workmanship, and request a receipt for significant purchases rather than assuming every sales claim is independently certified.

## Day two: choose Chahai or Haitang Mountain

For archaeology, arrange a visit to 查海遗址博物馆. Liaoning's culture and tourism authority highlights Chahai's early settlement evidence and stone-piled dragon image. Confirm whether the museum, site, or both are open; research facilities and protected ground may have different access rules.

For a landscape-and-culture day, choose 海棠山 (Haitang Mountain). The provincial tourism authority describes a protected mountain setting with Puan Temple and cliff carvings. Follow religious etiquette, do not touch carvings, avoid feeding animals, and obey forest-fire and trail controls.

## Practical planning

- Verify the exact Fuxin rail station and current services through [China Railway 12306](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Confirm the mine park, Chahai museum, and Haitang Mountain separately; opening status at one does not confirm another.
- Use a licensed driver for outlying sites and agree on the return journey in advance.
- Carry your passport and ask about registration and photography rules.
- Check the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) plus local fire, wind, ice, and road notices.
- Never enter active, abandoned, or rehabilitating industrial land without explicit visitor access.
