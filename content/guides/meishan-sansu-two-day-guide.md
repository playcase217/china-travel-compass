Meishan is the home city of Su Xun, Su Shi, and Su Zhe—the celebrated “Three Su”—and it deserves more than a rushed stop from Chengdu. Two days allow time for the Three Su Shrine Museum, a literature-led city walk, local food context, and one modest excursion without confusing later commemoration with Song-dynasty remains.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** literature, museum gardens, calligraphy, food history, and slow city breaks.
- **Time needed:** two days; distant mountain areas require a separate trip.
- **Simple plan:** Three Su Shrine and central Meishan; one city museum, wetland walk, or verified nearby cultural stop.
- **Main planning issue:** “Meishan” also names a large prefecture whose headline sights are far apart.
- **Editorial view:** read the writers' works and later memory together, while keeping evidence and legend distinct.

## Day one: give the Three Su Shrine time

Begin at the Three Su Shrine Museum after confirming its current opening day, reservation method, and passport procedure. The complex combines museum interpretation, a classical garden setting, memorial spaces, and later architecture. Read construction and restoration dates carefully rather than assuming every hall survives from the Northern Song.

Allow time for exhibitions on the family's writing, public careers, calligraphy, and reception across later centuries. English interpretation may be limited, so download translations or short public-domain excerpts beforehand. Do not touch steles, use flash where prohibited, or obstruct narrow rooms with tripods.

Spend the late afternoon on a compact central walk through officially open historic streets, wall remains, or public waterfront space. Commercial “Song-style” settings can be enjoyable, but they are contemporary interpretation and entertainment.

## Day two: choose one supporting theme

Stay in the city for a museum, cultural venue, and the Dongpo urban wetland area, or arrange one verified nearby visit. Official Sichuan routes connect Meishan's literary story with food culture and Qing神's bamboo traditions, but Qing神 is a separate transfer and a workshop should never be assumed to accept walk-ins.

Food names associated with Su Shi often mix documented history, later tradition, and modern branding. Enjoy them as regional cuisine, ask about ingredients and allergens, and be cautious about claims that a particular restaurant or recipe is uniquely “authentic.”

Do not add Wawu Mountain, Liujiang Ancient Town, or another distant county attraction to this two-day city plan. Those need their own weather, road, accommodation, and return-transport checks.

## Practical planning

- Search 眉山 for Meishan and 三苏祠 for the Three Su Shrine.
- Confirm the exact station and train through the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Carry your passport, a charged phone, rain or sun protection, and a payment backup.
- Ask before photographing worship, craft work, staff, or residents in non-museum spaces.

Meishan pairs naturally with [Chengdu](/guides/chengdu-two-day-itinerary/) or [Leshan](/guides/leshan-giant-buddha-day-trip/) once current rail times are verified.
