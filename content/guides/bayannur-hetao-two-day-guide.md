Bayannur is a useful place to understand how the Yellow River, irrigation, farming, wetlands, and the Yinshan landscape fit together. The municipality is enormous, so base in Linhe and choose a compact water-heritage plan rather than attempting the desert, grassland, rock art, and border region in two days.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** water engineering, agricultural landscapes, Yellow River history, and regional museums.
- **Time needed:** two days with prearranged road transport.
- **Simple plan:** Linhe museum and canal context; one official Yellow River or irrigation-heritage area.
- **Main planning issue:** long distances and active infrastructure that is not automatically open to visitors.
- **Editorial view:** read the Hetao plain as a managed water system, not simply a green exception inside arid Inner Mongolia.

## Day one: establish the Hetao story

Start with a confirmed-open municipal, irrigation, or Yellow River museum in the Linhe area. Bayannur's official history page explains that Yellow River, farming, and grassland cultures meet here, while the Hetao Irrigation District entered the World Irrigation Heritage list in 2019.

Use exhibits to distinguish the river, main canals, drainage works, lakes, fields, and later engineering. Avoid reducing the landscape to an ancient miracle: it is a living system that requires allocation, maintenance, flood planning, drainage, and conservation.

Walk only along designated public sections of canals or waterfront. Embankments, gates, pumping stations, and control buildings may be operational infrastructure with restricted access.

## Day two: one verified water landscape

Choose one official visitor facility connected with the Yellow River or Hetao system after confirming its gate and opening status. Municipal planning documents identify museums and interpretation facilities connected with Yellow River water culture, but a planning reference does not guarantee daily public access.

Arrange a licensed driver, provide the exact Chinese destination, and agree on waiting and return terms. Do not ask to stop on canal service roads, active levees, farm tracks, or wetland habitat. If your choice involves Wuliangsuhai or another protected water body, use a staffed public entrance and obey wildlife and boat controls.

## Weather, food, and respectful travel

- Spring wind, summer heat and storms, and severe winter cold can all change a sensible itinerary.
- Agricultural roads may be busy with machinery; never enter fields or photograph workers closely without permission.
- Ask how lamb, fish, noodles, and shared dishes are priced and portioned before ordering.
- Treat grassland and Mongolian cultural experiences as community life, not costumes available on demand.

## Getting there

Search 巴彦淖尔, 临河, 河套灌区, and your exact venue. Check rail services through the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/) and confirm that your lodging is in the intended district. Pair the trip with [Hohhot](/guides/hohhot-two-day-itinerary/) only as a separate stop.
