Lianyungang's appeal comes from the contrast between Huaguoshan and the Yellow Sea, but the city, mountain entrances, port districts, and island attractions are not one walkable cluster. Give each day one geographic focus.

The simplest first trip is Huaguoshan on the better hiking-weather day and one confirmed-open coastal area on the other. Keep alternatives ready for high wind, heavy rain, heat, or marine closures.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** mountain walks, *Journey to the West* cultural associations, coastlines, and regional rail trips.
- **Time needed:** two full days.
- **Simple plan:** Huaguoshan; Liandao or a Lianyun coastal district.
- **Main planning issue:** spread-out attractions, mountain conditions, and weather-dependent coastal access.
- **Editorial view:** enjoy the literary layer without presenting the novel's mythology as archaeological history.

## Day one: Huaguoshan at a sensible pace

Confirm the operating entrance, ticket channel, shuttle or cableway status, last descent, and any weather restrictions before leaving the city. Choose a route for your fitness and footwear rather than copying a highlight list. Stone steps can become slippery, and summer heat can turn a short climb into a serious effort.

The mountain is strongly associated in tourism with *Journey to the West*. Treat named caves, rocks, and Monkey King imagery as part of a living literary and popular-culture landscape. Follow official signs, keep away from cliff edges, and never feed or approach macaques or other wildlife.

## Day two: one coastal zone

The municipal government's sample itineraries pair Huaguoshan with places such as Liandao, Lianyun Old Street, and coastal mountain areas. Select one cluster only after checking its current access. Beach swimming, boats, island roads, and waterfront paths may close because of wind, waves, maintenance, or seasonal controls.

If Liandao is operating, establish the correct transport and public swimming areas on arrival. If conditions are poor, use a museum, historic street, or staffed scenic area inland rather than trying to bypass a marine closure.

Buy seafood only from businesses that clearly display prices and ask how an item is sold—by dish, portion, or weight—before ordering.

## Transport and onward planning

- Save Lianyungang as 连云港, Huaguoshan as 花果山, Liandao as 连岛, and Lianyun Old Street as 连云老街.
- Use the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/) for train planning.
- Confirm the return ride before staying for sunset in a remote coastal area.
- Continue separately to [Nanjing](/guides/nanjing-two-day-itinerary/) or [Yancheng](/guides/yancheng-yellow-sea-wetlands-two-day-guide/).
