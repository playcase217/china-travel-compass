Erenhot, also written Erlian or Erlianhot, is an Inner Mongolia border city with an unusual combination of dinosaur heritage, steppe landscapes, and the overland Tea Road story. Two days are enough for its core attractions if you verify access first. Being in a border tourism city does not grant permission to approach every border road, facility, or viewpoint.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** paleontology, overland-route history, open landscapes, and unusual rail journeys.
- **Time needed:** two days, allowing for weather and local transfers.
- **Simple plan:** dinosaur museum or geopark first, then a confirmed-open Tea Road or border-themed venue.
- **Main planning issue:** museums, fossil sites, grassland areas, and border attractions have distinct tickets and access rules.
- **Editorial view:** the fossils are the strongest reason to visit; the border should be approached as a regulated setting, not a photo challenge.

## Day one: dinosaurs with scientific context

Start at the currently operating dinosaur museum, geopark visitor facility, or fossil-protection display. Erenhot's municipal plan identifies the national geopark, dinosaur fossil nature reserve, and dinosaur museum as separate parts of its tourism landscape. Confirm which facility holds the main exhibition and whether outdoor areas are open.

Read exhibits before walking outdoor trails so reconstructed animals, excavation localities, and original fossils do not blur together. Never collect rock or fossil material, cross a barrier, or touch an exposed specimen. Wind and sun can be intense even when the air feels cool; carry water and eye protection.

## Day two: Tea Road history and regulated frontiers

Choose a confirmed-open museum or heritage site connected with the historic relay and trade routes, such as the Yilin Post Station story, then add a legal public steppe viewpoint if conditions allow. Stay on established roads and respect grazing land, fire controls, wildlife, and private or collectively managed pasture.

If a border-gate attraction is open to ordinary visitors, follow its current passport, ticket, photography, and security instructions exactly. Do not photograph checkpoints, personnel, surveillance equipment, rail operations, or other sensitive infrastructure unless clearly permitted. A domestic visit does not authorize crossing into Mongolia; international entry and exit require separate lawful documents and a functioning port of entry.

## Practical planning

- Search both **Erenhot** and **Erlian** when checking official information, but use the exact station shown on [China Railway 12306](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Confirm museum, geopark, fossil-area, and border-attraction access independently; do not assume a combined ticket.
- Carry your passport at all times and accept that access may be refused or suspended.
- Arrange licensed transport for outlying sites and avoid unsupervised off-road travel.
- Check the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) for high wind, dust, cold, heat, and storms.
