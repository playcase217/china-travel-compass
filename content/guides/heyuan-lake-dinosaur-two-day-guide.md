Heyuan offers a pairing rarely found in one small Guangdong city: a museum story built around dinosaur fossils and a large reservoir landscape at Wanlü Lake. Two days work well if you keep the museum and lake on separate days and verify the boat or scenic-area plan before leaving town.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** natural history, family travel, reservoir scenery, and a quieter Guangdong stop.
- **Time needed:** two days.
- **Simple plan:** dinosaur museum and Guifeng Pagoda; then one managed Wanlü Lake outing.
- **Main planning issue:** lake attractions can involve separate gates, boats, and weather-dependent operations.
- **Editorial view:** do not promise children a boat trip until the operator confirms that day's service.

## Day one: fossils and the old city landmark

Start with 河源市博物馆（河源恐龙博物馆）. The municipal government describes the institution as responsible for local history and folk collections as well as research and protection of Heyuan's dinosaur resources. Treat popular “world record” language cautiously and let the current displays explain what was found, where it came from, and how fossils are conserved.

The museum also manages 龟峰塔 (Guifeng Pagoda). If the pagoda precinct is open, pair it with the museum rather than trying to cross the city repeatedly. Follow staff directions about climbing, photography, and protected areas. Finish with a central meal and ask about Hakka dishes without assuming every restaurant uses the same recipe or English name.

## Day two: choose a managed Wanlü Lake experience

万绿湖 (Wanlü Lake) is the scenic name associated with the Xinfengjiang reservoir. Choose one official, operating scenic entrance and confirm whether your ticket includes a boat, islands, shuttle, or only shore access. Reservoir water levels, maintenance, thunderstorms, and strong wind can alter the plan.

Wear sun protection, carry water, and use a life jacket whenever instructed. Do not swim, enter restricted dam infrastructure, or follow informal tracks to the water. If boats are suspended, use a confirmed-open shore viewpoint or return to the city rather than improvising an unsafe excursion.

## Practical planning

- Distinguish 河源站 and 河源东站 when booking and arranging a hotel transfer.
- Use the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/) for current train information.
- Carry your passport and confirm whether the museum or lake uses timed or identity-based tickets.
- Check the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) before a lake day.
- Ask the scenic area to confirm the exact entrance, return transport, last boat, and weather policy.
- Seasonal extended museum hours are temporary; never carry them into a later trip without rechecking.
