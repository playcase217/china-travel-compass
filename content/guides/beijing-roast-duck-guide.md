Beijing roast duck is worth planning as one memorable meal rather than treating it as a quick snack. The classic experience centers on crisp skin, tender slices of duck, thin pancakes, scallion, cucumber, and sauce, often with carving presented at the table.

## At a glance

Reserve ahead for a popular restaurant, especially at dinner or on weekends. A whole duck is best shared, so ask about portion size if you are traveling solo or as a pair. Expect a relaxed meal, and confirm the final price and any service charges when ordering.

## What arrives at the table

The familiar combination is sliced roast duck served with pancakes and accompaniments. Place a small amount of duck, sauce, scallion, and cucumber inside a pancake, then fold it into a manageable bite.

Restaurants vary in style. Some emphasize a polished dining-room experience; others feel more casual. The best choice depends on whether you want a landmark meal, convenience, or a neighborhood setting.

## How much to order

A whole duck is usually designed for sharing. If your group is small, ask whether a half portion is available and avoid ordering too many additional dishes before you understand the serving size.

For a broader meal, add vegetables and one or two lighter dishes rather than creating an overly heavy table.

## When to eat it

Dinner works well after a day of sightseeing, but lunch can be easier to reserve. If you are visiting the [Forbidden City](/guides/forbidden-city-beijing/) and [Tiananmen Square](/guides/tiananmen-square-beijing/), a roast duck meal is a satisfying finish to a central Beijing day.

## Practical restaurant tips

- Reserve ahead for well-known restaurants.
- Ask about whole and half portions.
- Confirm whether additional accompaniments are charged separately.
- Keep a backup payment method.
- Save the restaurant name and address in Chinese.
- Mention dietary restrictions clearly before ordering.

## Add food without overplanning

Roast duck can be your signature Beijing meal, but leave space for simpler experiences too: breakfast stalls, noodles, dumplings, and local snacks often become equally memorable.
