Kongtong Mountain is the reason to stop in Pingliang, but it should be treated as a full mountain day rather than a quick roadside sight. Base yourself in the city, confirm the current entrance and transport system, then give the temples, cliff paths, and forest enough time.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** mountain scenery, religious architecture, geology, and travelers crossing eastern Gansu.
- **Time needed:** two days, including arrival and one full mountain day.
- **Simple plan:** Pingliang orientation; Kongtong Mountain circuit; departure after a weather buffer.
- **Main planning issue:** routes combine steep paths with scenic transport whose operation can change.
- **Editorial view:** choose a modest open circuit and read the mountain as both a living religious place and a protected landscape.

## Day one: arrive and verify the route

Settle in Pingliang and use the afternoon for a low-key city walk. Save 平凉 and 崆峒山 in Chinese, then confirm the correct visitor entrance, real-name ticket procedure, passport handling, first and last scenic transport, and any closed paths through the official scenic-area channel.

The municipal overview describes a mountain of conglomerate cliffs, forest, temples, and elevations above 2,100 meters. That variety explains why a route that looks short on a map may involve sustained climbing. Decide in advance whether your priority is architecture, a summit viewpoint, or a gentler forest walk; trying to combine every branch is a poor first visit.

## Day two: one coherent mountain circuit

Start early and follow the route currently designated by the operator. Historic buildings and active religious spaces require quiet behavior: do not enter closed rooms, touch statuary, use flash where prohibited, or photograph worshippers without permission. Keep food and smoke away from wooden structures and forest.

Cliff paths, polished stone, fog, wind, ice, and summer storms can change the difficulty quickly. Wear shoes with grip, carry water and layers, and turn back before an uncertain final connection. A scenic vehicle or ropeway should be treated as optional infrastructure, not a rescue plan guaranteed to operate.

The official Pingliang site posted a 2026 seasonal notice with specific fares and hours, but those details can expire. Use it only to understand that seasons and transport products are managed separately; check the current notice rather than copying an old price into your budget.

## Getting there and onward

Check rail services on the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/). Match the station name on your ticket rather than searching only “Pingliang,” and leave a large buffer after the mountain before any reserved departure.

Use the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) plus the scenic area's same-day notice for rain, lightning, snow, wind, heat, and fire controls. Never use an unofficial forest track or climb around a locked gate.
