Qinzhou makes an unusual Guangxi pairing: one day learning about Nixing pottery and the port city's history, then one carefully planned day on the Beibu Gulf coast. The craft venues, central city, and Sanniang Bay are not one walkable attraction.

Keep wildlife expectations modest. A coastal visit can be worthwhile for villages, rocks, weather, and seascape even when no dolphin appears.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** ceramics, working-city history, coastal landscapes, and travelers continuing through southern Guangxi.
- **Time needed:** two days with an overnight.
- **Simple plan:** pottery museum or traceable workshop and old streets; then one official coastal destination.
- **Main planning issue:** workshop access, rural road transfers, tides, storms, boat safety, and wildlife marketing.
- **Editorial view:** make pottery the guaranteed cultural focus and treat any wildlife sighting as an unpromised bonus.

## Day one: understand Nixing pottery

Choose a confirmed-open museum, visitor center, or identifiable workshop that explains clay preparation, forming, carving, firing, and finishing. A retail display alone may not explain which stages were completed locally or by whom.

If joining a workshop, confirm the language, duration, safety equipment, firing or shipping arrangements, and total price before starting. Do not enter production areas or touch tools and kilns without permission.

When buying, request the maker or producer, material and process description, price, receipt, and safe packing. Color variation and a dramatic origin story do not by themselves prove age, authorship, or quality. Avoid investment claims and arrange international shipping only through a traceable provider that explains customs responsibility.

Use the remaining time for a public old-street or museum route, respecting homes, worship, and active businesses.

## Day two: coast with realistic expectations

Travel to one officially operating coastal area such as Sanniang Bay only after confirming road access, weather, tide-related conditions, return transport, and available facilities. The coast can face heat, lightning, rough water, wind, and temporary controls.

If considering a boat, use a licensed operator at an official departure point. Confirm the approved route, passenger capacity, life jackets, weather cancellation policy, and return. Never pressure a crew to chase, surround, feed, touch, or approach dolphins closely. Sightings are never guaranteed, and responsible operators should prioritize distance and animal behavior over photographs.

Do not swim outside a designated, staffed area or climb wet rocks for a better view. Carry water and sun protection, and keep enough time for the road back to Qinzhou.

## Arrival and onward travel

- Search 钦州 for Qinzhou, 坭兴陶 for Nixing pottery, and 三娘湾 for Sanniang Bay.
- Confirm trains and station names through the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Book central lodging for the pottery day, then arrange the coastal transfer separately.
- Keep the coast off the itinerary when official warnings or local controls advise against travel.

Qinzhou pairs naturally with [Nanning](/guides/nanning-two-day-itinerary/) or [Beihai](/guides/beihai-two-day-coastal-guide/), subject to current transport checks.
