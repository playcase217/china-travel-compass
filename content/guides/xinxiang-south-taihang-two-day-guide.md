The South Taihang landscapes administered from Xinxiang are a road-and-trail trip, not an easy extension of downtown sightseeing. In two days, pair **Wanxianshan and Guoliang** with one officially open canyon area such as Baligou, while accepting that rain, ice, rockfall risk, and shuttle controls may force a simpler plan.

Guoliang's cliff-side road is famous, but it is also working mountain infrastructure inside a managed scenic area. Visit for the engineering story and landscape; do not treat the tunnel as a stunt-driving location.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** dramatic geology, engineered mountain roads, village landscapes, and prepared walkers.
- **Time needed:** two full days from a mountain-area base; add transfer time from Xinxiang.
- **Simple plan:** Wanxianshan and Guoliang; one managed Baligou-area route.
- **Main planning issue:** scenic areas use their own entrances and shuttle systems, and mountain access can change quickly.
- **Editorial view:** do not combine every South Taihang brand name. One complete trail is better than hours spent transferring between gates.

## Day one: Wanxianshan and Guoliang

Enter through the official **Wanxianshan** visitor system and follow the current shuttle plan. The Xinxiang culture and tourism bureau identifies Guoliang village and its cliff road as core attractions within the area. Ask staff which walking segments are open and where pedestrians may safely view the road.

Inside the tunnel, stay within marked pedestrian space, remain alert for vehicles, and never step toward an opening for a photograph without checking both directions. Do not stop a private car, lean beyond barriers, climb cliff edges, or enter unsigned tracks.

In the village, remember that homes and service businesses coexist with the attraction. Ask before photographing residents or courtyards and check prices before meals or local transport.

## Day two: choose one managed canyon route

Use the second day for a currently open route at **Baligou**, Tianjieshan, Jiulianshan, Guanshan, or another official South Taihang scenic area. The municipal tourism bureau's published hiking routes distinguish waterfalls, canyon paths, geological interpretation, cliff walks, and historic themes; choose according to fitness and current conditions.

At the gate, verify total walking distance, elevation change, stairs, shuttle or cableway status, last return, and emergency closures. Waterfall flow varies, and a dry forecast in Xinxiang city does not prove a mountain trail is safe. Turn back when staff close a route.

## Practical planning

- Search Xinxiang and Huixian separately on [China Railway 12306](https://www.12306.cn/en/), then confirm the road transfer to the exact scenic-area gate.
- Save 新乡, 辉县, 万仙山, 郭亮村, 郭亮洞, and 八里沟 in Chinese.
- Book a licensed transfer or verify the current tourist-bus departure point; do not assume an app car will accept a distant mountain pickup.
- Wear grippy footwear and carry water, sun protection, and a warm or waterproof layer.
- Check the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) for heavy rain, thunderstorms, heat, fog, snow, and ice.
