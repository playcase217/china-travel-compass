Danzhou gives western Hainan a strong literary anchor. Su Shi spent three years in the area during exile, and Dongpo Academy preserves the most accessible setting for understanding that period. Two days can connect the academy and Zhonghe with local culture and one additional landscape without pretending that Danzhou's widely scattered attractions form a walkable resort.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** literary history, Song-era cultural memory, historic buildings, local food, and a quieter Hainan itinerary.
- **Time needed:** two days with road transport.
- **Simple plan:** Dongpo Academy and Zhonghe; then a museum or one confirmed geological, botanical, or coastal extension.
- **Main planning issue:** Danzhou, Yangpu, Zhonghe, Baimajing, and outlying scenic areas are distinct clusters.
- **Editorial view:** keep Su Shi's Hainan years at the center and choose only one second-day extension.

## Day one: Dongpo Academy and Zhonghe

Begin at 东坡书院 (Dongpo Academy), whose earlier Zaijiu Hall tradition is associated with Su Shi's teaching during his Hainan exile. Read the architecture, inscriptions, collections, reconstructed elements, and later commemoration together; the site has evolved across centuries and should not be presented as an untouched Song-dynasty campus.

Allow time for any official exhibition or guided interpretation. Performances and immersive programs can help visitors enter the story, but schedules vary and dramatization is not the same as a primary historical source.

Continue through the public lanes of 中和古镇 (Zhonghe Ancient Town) if current access is straightforward. Respect residents, temples, schools, and private courtyards. Do not photograph people through doorways or enter buildings simply because a gate is open.

## Day two: choose one Danzhou extension

For a culture-focused day, select a confirmed-open Danzhou museum, intangible-heritage venue, or another officially interpreted Dongpo site. Ask before recording 儋州调声 (Danzhou diaosheng) or craft demonstrations, and avoid treating living traditions as performances available on demand.

For landscape, choose one officially open destination such as a geological, botanical, reservoir, salt-field, or coastal site listed by the provincial or municipal tourism authority. Check travel time carefully. Yangpu includes working port and industrial zones, so stay on public visitor routes and never enter docks, salt works, quarries, caves, research grounds, or shore facilities without explicit permission.

## Practical planning

- Verify the exact rail station and current trains on [China Railway 12306](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Use licensed road transport and group sites by geography rather than by online popularity.
- Carry your passport and confirm reservations, opening days, performances, and guided visits.
- Check the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) and official Hainan notices for heat, lightning, heavy rain, typhoons, and coastal conditions.
- Use sun protection and water year-round; stop outdoor travel when heat or storm warnings require it.
- Treat caves, salt fields, beaches, ports, reservoirs, and botanical venues as controlled sites with changeable access.
