Xinyu is a compact Jiangxi base for Fairy Lake, a reservoir landscape promoted for islands, water scenery, and its association with the celestial-maiden story in *In Search of the Supernatural*. Treat it as a managed water destination rather than an open-access wilderness.

Two days provide a weather buffer: orient yourself in Xinyu first, then give the lake a full day. Travelers interested only in a quick photograph may find the transfer and boat logistics disproportionate.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** quiet lake scenery, regional folklore, photography, and slower Jiangxi travel.
- **Time needed:** two days including one dedicated lake day.
- **Simple plan:** Xinyu city context; Fairy Lake with a verified route.
- **Main planning issue:** changing boat circuits, heat, storms, and protected water areas.
- **Editorial view:** the strongest visit separates the documented landscape from modern romantic branding.

## Day one: Xinyu at an easy pace

Use arrival day for a currently open museum, park, or riverside public space. Confirm any museum's closed day, passport process, and final admission before crossing town. English interpretation may be limited, so save Chinese names and translate exhibit summaries without photographing restricted material.

Ask your accommodation or the official lake service channel to confirm the next day's recognized entrance, transport, ticketing, boat route, and last connection back. A listing that says “Fairy Lake” may point to a hotel, village, pier, or a distant shoreline rather than the main visitor entry.

## Day two: Fairy Lake

Go early enough to handle staffed ticketing with a passport. The scenic area's official website describes Fairy Lake as a national scenic area and links its name to a story recorded by the Eastern Jin writer Gan Bao. Approach folklore as cultural tradition, not literal history.

Choose only a currently offered official circuit. Boats and island stops may change with water levels, wind, storms, maintenance, crowd controls, or conservation needs. Check the return time before boarding, wear the supplied life jacket correctly, and never pressure staff to sail in unsafe conditions.

Stay on marked paths at island stops. Do not swim, fish, launch a drone, enter closed woodland, or leave litter unless current signs explicitly allow the activity. Large reservoirs can have steep edges and rapidly changing weather even when the city feels calm.

## Practical planning

- Save 新余 for Xinyu and 仙女湖风景名胜区 for the scenic-area entrance.
- Confirm rail travel through the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Bring your passport, water, sun and rain protection, motion-sickness supplies if needed, and a payment backup.
- Check the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) and local warnings before committing to a boat day.
- Avoid fixed online claims about ticket bundles or island sequences; buy only the route you understand.

Xinyu can connect with [Nanchang](/guides/nanchang-two-day-itinerary/) or [Jingdezhen](/guides/jingdezhen-porcelain-guide/) on a wider Jiangxi trip, with current rail times checked separately.
