Golmud is a transport and industrial city in the Qaidam Basin, not a quick gateway to every landscape shown under the broad “Kunlun” label. A responsible two-day visit keeps one day for city history and one for a managed Qarhan Salt Lake excursion, with altitude, distance, weather, and industrial controls built into the plan.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** salt-lake geology, high-plateau infrastructure, industrial landscapes, and modern transport history.
- **Time needed:** two days after acclimatizing; add time rather than rushing onward.
- **Simple plan:** city and Qinghai–Tibet road history; then the official Qarhan visitor area.
- **Main planning issue:** altitude, dehydration, ultraviolet exposure, long road distances, and controlled industrial land.
- **Editorial view:** the safest itinerary is also the most informative: one authorized salt-lake visit beats a chase for unsigned “secret” viewpoints.

## Day one: understand the city before the landscape

Start at 将军楼公园 (General's Building Park) or another confirmed-open public museum or cultural venue. The city government presents Golmud as a modern city shaped by the Qinghai–Tibet Highway and resource development. Reading that history first makes the rail lines, industrial zones, and desert-edge settlement more meaningful.

Keep the first afternoon light. Golmud's urban elevation is already high enough for some visitors to feel headache, fatigue, poor sleep, or nausea. Rest, hydrate normally, avoid hard exercise and alcohol, and seek medical help if symptoms become severe or worsen. Do not plan a higher mountain excursion as your first activity.

## Day two: Qarhan Salt Lake through the official entrance

Visit 察尔汗盐湖 (Qarhan Salt Lake) only through its currently operating scenic-area entrance or an accountable operator. Golmud's official site describes the visitor area as part of a vast salt-lake and industrial landscape south of the city. The enormous wider lake is not unrestricted recreation land.

Follow staff and boardwalk instructions. Do not drive onto salt crust, enter brine, collect salt crystals, approach machinery, cross rail or service roads, or walk toward a distant reflective surface. Salt crust can conceal water or unstable ground, while industrial traffic and glare add risk. Confirm the last return to Golmud before entering.

## Transport and high-plateau safety

Verify trains and the exact station through the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/). For the salt lake, confirm the current transfer method, pickup point, passport acceptance, and return directly with the scenic area or your accommodation. Avoid informal long-distance rides with no documented return plan.

Use the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) for high wind, cold, snow, thunderstorms, and ultraviolet conditions. Carry water, sun protection, warm layers, and offline Chinese place names. A road visible on a map may serve a mine, railway, reserve, or military-controlled area; never bypass barriers or follow social-media coordinates off an authorized route.
