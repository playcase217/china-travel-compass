Bazhong and nearby Enyang offer a compact introduction to northeastern Sichuan through two different forms of heritage: rock-cut religious art and a lived-in market town associated with the historic Miancang routes. Two days are enough if each receives its own day and transport is confirmed in advance.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** grotto art, historic roads, old-town life, riverside walks, and regional history.
- **Time needed:** two days with one transfer between Bazhong and Enyang.
- **Simple plan:** one officially open grotto or museum route; then Enyang old town.
- **Main planning issue:** grotto groups are separate protected sites, not one continuous attraction.
- **Editorial view:** conservation comes before close-up photography or completing a checklist of caves.

## Day one: Bazhong's carvings

Choose one officially open and staffed site such as the authorized visitor route at the Nankan grottoes, after confirming current access. Bazhong's official cultural plan treats Nankan, Beikan, Shuining Temple, Xikan, and Shimen Temple as related but distinct grotto locations; do not assume they can all be visited in one day.

Use a museum or official guide to understand dating, iconography, conservation, and later repairs. Never touch, trace, wet, illuminate aggressively, or squeeze past a barrier for a photograph. Follow restrictions on tripods and flash, and leave immediately if staff close an area for weather or conservation.

## Day two: Enyang old town

Walk Enyang's historic lanes and riverside spaces slowly. Official planning emphasizes the town's Miancang Road and trading history alongside living neighborhoods. Distinguish older structures and revolutionary-history sites from newly built entertainment streets and digital attractions.

Current evening programs can be enjoyable, but shows, projections, and boat experiences may be seasonal or ticketed. Confirm them the same day and keep a normal street walk as the core plan. Ask before photographing residents, and do not block doors or working lanes.

## Practical planning

- Search 巴中 for Bazhong, 恩阳古镇 for Enyang Old Town, and 南龛石窟 for Nankan Grottoes.
- Confirm rail and station details through the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Ask your accommodation to verify the current bus or car return before leaving either urban center.
- Carry your passport, shoes with grip, rain protection, and a payment backup.

Bazhong can fit into a longer Sichuan journey after [Chengdu](/guides/chengdu-two-day-itinerary/), but it is not a casual day trip.
