Neijiang is an easy place to slow down between the better-known cities of the Sichuan Basin. Its “Sweet City” identity grew from sugar production, while nearby Zizhong preserves a notably close pairing of civil and martial temples. The best first visit is not a rushed attraction circuit: give one day to Neijiang and one to Zizhong.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** regional history, temple architecture, Sichuan food, and travelers who enjoy secondary cities.
- **Time needed:** two days, sleeping in Neijiang or Zizhong according to your onward train.
- **Simple plan:** Neijiang museum and river-city context; then Zizhong's old-town heritage.
- **Main planning issue:** Neijiang and Zizhong have separate railway stations and local transport networks.
- **Editorial view:** treat Zizhong as a full day, not a brief stop between trains.

## Day one: understand Neijiang

Begin at a confirmed-open municipal museum or public cultural venue. The goal is to understand the city's river setting, transport links, agriculture, and the history behind the “Sweet City” name before walking through the modern center. Exhibitions rotate, so check the current venue and reservation channel rather than relying on an old listing.

Continue with an established public walk beside the Tuo River or through a central neighborhood. Use signed paths and respect flood or construction barriers. This is a good day for an unhurried meal: ask hotel staff to write down a few local dishes, check prices before ordering, and be clear about chili and dietary restrictions.

Do not add distant county attractions simply because they appear under “Neijiang” in an app. The municipality covers a wider area than the compact urban core.

## Day two: Zizhong's paired temples and old streets

Travel to 资中 (Zizhong) and make the 文庙 (Confucian Temple) and 武庙 (Martial Temple) the anchors of the day. The Sichuan cultural-heritage authority notes that the two complexes stand only about 100 meters apart, an unusual urban pairing whose contrasting roof colors are part of Zizhong's historic character.

Walk the surrounding old-city streets only where access is clearly public. Preservation work can close individual halls or lanes, and religious or ceremonial areas may have different photography rules. 罗泉古镇 (Luoquan Ancient Town) is a separate outing; add it only after confirming same-day transport and enough return time.

## Practical planning

- Search 内江站, 内江北站, and 资中北站 separately when planning trains.
- Check rail services on the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Carry your passport; museums and heritage sites may use identity-based admission.
- Save 内江市博物馆, 资中文庙, and 资中武庙 in Chinese.
- Check the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) and local flood notices in the rainy season.
- Confirm temple access, restoration closures, and last admission through official local channels shortly before visiting.
