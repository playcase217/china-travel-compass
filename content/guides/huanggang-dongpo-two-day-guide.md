For a literary traveler, Huanggang's Huangzhou district is inseparable from Su Shi, also known as Su Dongpo. His years here shaped famous prose and poetry, while today's city links Dongpo Red Cliff, Yiai Lake, parks, and cultural venues into a manageable urban visit. Two days leave room to distinguish literary memory from the physical history of each site.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** classical literature, gardens, cultural history, and relaxed city walking.
- **Time needed:** two days in Huangzhou.
- **Simple plan:** Dongpo Red Cliff and museum context; then Yiai Lake and nearby cultural spaces.
- **Main planning issue:** Huanggang has several stations serving different districts and counties.
- **Editorial view:** read a short translation of Su Shi before arrival, then let the landscape add context.

## Day one: Dongpo Red Cliff and historical context

Begin at 东坡赤壁 (Dongpo Red Cliff), sometimes called the “literary Red Cliff.” It should not be confused with the better-known battlefield tradition associated with Chibi farther west. Official Hubei tourism material connects Huangzhou's site with works Su Shi composed during his time here, including the two Red Cliff rhapsodies and the famous lyric recalling the river.

Use the site's current interpretation rather than assuming every structure dates to Su Shi's lifetime. Pair the visit with a confirmed-open museum or cultural venue in Huangzhou, then walk only on established riverfront routes. Flood controls, construction, and high water can close sections without much notice.

## Day two: Yiai Lake and the living city

Spend the second day around 遗爱湖 (Yiai Lake). The name draws on the idea of a public official whose good governance remains cherished after departure, and the modern park uses Dongpo culture as part of its identity. It is also an everyday urban landscape, so leave time for residents' routines rather than treating it as a sequence of photo stops.

If open, add the Yiai Lake Art Museum or another public gallery. Exhibitions and event schedules change, especially around festivals. Finish with a meal in Huangzhou and treat dishes branded with “Dongpo” as living culinary interpretations, not automatic evidence of a single unbroken recipe.

## Practical planning

- Check 黄冈西站, 黄冈站, and 黄冈东站 carefully; use the station printed on your ticket.
- Verify trains with the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Save 东坡赤壁 and 遗爱湖 in Chinese for navigation.
- Carry your passport and confirm attraction reservation or identity requirements.
- Check the [China Meteorological Administration](https://www.cma.gov.cn/en/) and local riverfront notices.
- Festival performances, night tours, promotions, and extended hours are not permanent services.
