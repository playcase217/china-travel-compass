Kaifeng is one of China's great old capitals, but it is often overlooked by international visitors who focus only on Beijing, Xi'an, and Shanghai. The city is known for Song Dynasty history, temples, old city atmosphere, night markets, and Henan food. It works well as a cultural stop with [Luoyang](/guides/luoyang-longmen-grottoes-guide/) or Zhengzhou.

This two-day itinerary keeps Kaifeng practical for English-speaking travelers. It focuses on history, food, and a compact route rather than trying to cover every reconstructed attraction.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** history lovers, food travelers, old-capital enthusiasts, families, and rail travelers in Henan.
- **Time needed:** two days; one long day works if you focus on a few central sites and a night market.
- **Simple plan:** Day 1 for old capital sites and local food. Day 2 for temples, museums, city walls, or a slower market route.
- **Main planning issue:** some attractions are reconstructed or theme-park-like, so choose based on your interest in atmosphere versus historical depth.
- **Useful before you go:** review [China high-speed rail basics](/guides/china-high-speed-rail-guide/) and prepare [China apps](/guides/china-apps-for-english-speakers/).

## Why Kaifeng Is Worth Visiting

Kaifeng's appeal is the combination of ancient capital history and everyday food culture. It may not feel as polished as China's biggest tourist cities, but that can make it more interesting for travelers who like street-level atmosphere.

The city is strongest when you combine one or two major heritage stops with local snacks, market energy, and a slower walk.

## Day 1: Song History And Night Market Food

Start with one major historic or Song-themed area. Check current ticketing and opening information before you go. If the site is partly reconstructed, treat it as an interpretive experience rather than untouched ancient fabric.

For lunch, look for Henan noodles, steamed buns, soups, or local snacks. Kaifeng is especially rewarding in the evening, when night markets and food streets become active. Try small portions and share dishes if traveling with others.

If you have dietary restrictions, translate them carefully. Broths, pork, beef, lamb, wheat, and sesame can appear in common foods.

## Day 2: Temples, City Walls, Or A Slower Old City Route

Use the second day for a temple, museum, city wall area, or another old-city walk. Choose two nearby places instead of spending the day in taxis between scattered attractions.

If you are continuing to Luoyang, Zhengzhou, or Xi'an, confirm your station and train time before lunch. Leave buffer time for traffic and station security.

## Practical Tips For English-Speaking Visitors

- Treat Kaifeng as a food-and-history city, not only a landmark checklist.
- Check whether attractions are historic, reconstructed, or performance-oriented.
- Save Chinese names for hotel, night market, station, and major sites.
- Prepare dietary notes for meat broths, pork, sesame, and wheat.
- Pair Kaifeng with Luoyang for a stronger Henan route.
- Keep evening food routes compact and easy to return from.
