Ordos is a large region of cities, grasslands, sandy landscapes, the Yellow River bend, museums, and cultural sites—not one continuous desert resort. Distances between Dongsheng, Kangbashi, Dalad, Hanggin, Ejin Horo, and Uxin areas are the central planning fact.

Allow three days and choose one landscape zone rather than collecting far-apart attractions. The Ordos municipal government lists grassland, desert, heritage, and urban destinations across different banners and districts, reinforcing the need to map the exact location first.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** open landscapes, desert ecology, Mongolian culture, museums, and regional road trips.
- **Time needed:** three days after reaching Ordos, with a licensed driver for remote areas.
- **Simple plan:** city orientation; one desert or sand-land day; one grassland, museum, or cultural day.
- **Main planning issue:** scale, seasonal operations, wind, heat or cold, and activity safety.
- **Editorial view:** one well-run landscape visit with ecological context is better than several entertainment compounds.

## Day one: understand the region

Begin in Dongsheng or Kangbashi and use a confirmed-open museum or interpretation venue to distinguish the Kubuqi Desert, Mu Us Sandy Land, grassland areas, and the region's Mongolian cultural history. These are not interchangeable labels.

Confirm the next two days with your lodging or licensed operator: exact Chinese destination, driving time, road surface, weather cancellation policy, insurance, emergency plan, meals, toilets, and return after dark. Do not accept an itinerary described only as “the desert.”

## Day two: choose one desert experience

Visit one authorized and currently operating desert or sandy-land area. Check which activities are included and whether each has age, health, weight, clothing, or weather restrictions. Wear sun and eye protection, carry water, secure electronics from sand, and prepare for rapid temperature changes.

Use helmets and restraints provided, refuse an unsafe vehicle or animal activity, and never pressure an animal handler to run faster for a video. Stay out of restoration plots and off closed dunes. Do not drive independently off road without legal access, suitable equipment, communications, and genuine local expertise.

## Day three: add grassland or cultural depth

Choose a grassland area, museum, or cultural site that fits the same geographic corridor. Grassland is productive and ecologically sensitive land, not an empty stage. Do not drive over vegetation, enter grazing areas, approach livestock, climb an ovoo, or imitate ceremonies without guidance.

Ask before photographing people, homes, religious practice, horses, or private gatherings. Verify what a performance represents and avoid presenting a resort show as spontaneous daily life.

## Transport and onward planning

- Save Ordos as 鄂尔多斯 and confirm the district or banner for every stop.
- Check trains only on the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/).
- Verify flights directly with the airline and airport.
- Use [Hohhot](/guides/hohhot-two-day-itinerary/) or [Yinchuan](/guides/yinchuan-two-day-itinerary/) as separate regional stops, not automatic day trips.
