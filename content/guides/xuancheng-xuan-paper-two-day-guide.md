Xuancheng is best approached as a craft region, not a single compact attraction zone. The city is associated with the wider culture of the Chinese scholar's studio, while the strongest Xuan-paper visit usually requires a separate trip toward Jing County. For an international visitor, two days are enough for context and one well-planned craft excursion.

Do not attempt Jing County, Jixi, Taohuatan, and every “four treasures” workshop in one weekend. Choose one production story and leave room to observe it carefully.

## At a glance

- **Best for:** paper, calligraphy, painting materials, museums, and southern Anhui landscapes.
- **Time needed:** two days for city context plus one county excursion.
- **Simple plan:** Xuancheng museum and cultural spaces; then a Xuan-paper route in Jing County.
- **Main planning issue:** dispersed counties, similarly named venues, workshop access, and uneven English interpretation.
- **Editorial view:** watch how raw material and repeated handwork become paper before shopping for finished souvenirs.

## Day one: understand the regional craft system

Start with an official museum, gallery, or cultural venue in Xuancheng. Look beyond famous product names to the relationships among paper, ink, brushes, inkstones, calligraphy, painting, publishing, and local trade. Photograph bilingual labels where permitted and use translation tools for specialist vocabulary.

Add one accessible city landscape such as Jingting Mountain only if weather and current access are suitable. A mountain walk and a detailed museum visit can fill most of a day; avoid squeezing in a distant county transfer afterward.

Before buying art materials, learn the difference between a regional tradition, a protected indication or recognized craft, a contemporary factory product, and a souvenir using the name loosely. Price alone does not establish age, fiber, method, or quality.

## Day two: choose one Xuan-paper route

Build the day around an official museum, recognized production site, or visitor center in Jing County. The Xuancheng government tourism directory lists the China Xuan Paper Culture Park among the region's visitor sites, but you should confirm the exact operating entity, reservation channel, demonstration schedule, and transport before departing.

During a demonstration, pay attention to the sequence rather than expecting every stage to run continuously for visitors. Ask before photographing workers, tools, drying areas, or proprietary processes. Workshops are workplaces; never touch pulp, screens, paper stacks, or drying walls without invitation.

If you shop, request written details about the product and keep expectations modest. “Handmade,” “traditional,” and “Xuan paper” can be used imprecisely in tourist retail. Buy because the sheet suits your intended calligraphy, painting, conservation, or gift purpose—not because a seller implies guaranteed collectibility.

## Getting around responsibly

- Search 宣城 for Xuancheng, 泾县 for Jing County, and 宣纸 for Xuan paper.
- Use the [official China Railway 12306 English site](https://www.12306.cn/en/) for current station and train choices.
- Confirm the last practical return before leaving the city; county buses and visitor shuttles can change.
- If hiring a car, agree on the Chinese venue name, waiting time, tolls, and total price in writing.

For a longer Anhui journey, combine Xuancheng with [Huangshan](/guides/huangshan-two-day-guide/) or [Xidi and Hongcun](/guides/xidi-hongcun-two-day-guide/) only with realistic transfer time.
