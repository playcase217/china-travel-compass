# SEO and GEO publishing plan

## Positioning

China Travel Compass serves international visitors who want practical, independent travel guidance for China. Articles should answer a real planning question clearly, add useful context that generic summaries omit, and link to official sources when facts may change.

## Topic clusters

Publish in focused clusters rather than creating many thin pages:

1. First-time essentials: entry policy, payments, mobile data, transport, hotel registration, essential apps.
2. City guides: Beijing, Shanghai, Xi'an, Chengdu, Hangzhou, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, and Yunnan routes.
3. Itineraries: 3-day, 7-day, and 14-day routes for distinct traveler needs.
4. Practical comparisons: high-speed rail versus flights, eSIM versus local SIM, and city combinations.

Each new article should link to relevant existing articles and one clear next step.

## Article quality checklist

- Use a specific traveler question as the article's primary purpose.
- Put a concise answer or recommendation near the top.
- Add original images, first-hand details, or a clearly explained editorial point of view.
- Separate durable advice from details that can change.
- Link policy, timetable, and booking claims to official sources.
- Include `published`, `updated`, `author`, `description`, `hero`, and descriptive `heroAlt` frontmatter.
- Review time-sensitive articles regularly and update the `updated` date.
- Avoid producing near-duplicate pages for keyword variations.

## Search engine onboarding

1. Verify `https://travel.juzhiic.com` in Google Search Console.
2. Submit `https://travel.juzhiic.com/sitemap.xml`.
3. Verify the site in Bing Webmaster Tools and submit the same sitemap.
4. Use Search Console URL Inspection after publishing important guides.
5. Track impressions, indexed pages, queries, and click-through rate monthly.

## Implemented technical foundation

- Crawlable static HTML with semantic article markup.
- Canonical URL, meta description, Open Graph, and Twitter card tags.
- `Article`, `BreadcrumbList`, `WebSite`, and `Organization` JSON-LD.
- XML sitemap with update dates and guide images.
- `robots.txt` with sitemap discovery.
- RSS feed for newly published guides.
- Visible breadcrumbs, editorial policy, bylines, update dates, and official source links.
